## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, mometasone is a potent corticosteroid that acts on the glucocorticoid receptor (NR3C1). Its effects include anti-inflammatory, immunosuppressive and antiproliferative actions.

Topical corticosteroids are a recognised skin-directed therapy for early-stage mycosis fungoides, the most common primary cutaneous T-cell lymphoma. They are thought to reduce inflammatory T-cell infiltrates and may induce lymphocyte apoptosis. This gives a plausible class-level rationale.

This reasoning comes from general corticosteroid knowledge, not from mometasone-specific data. The very high TxGNN score is a model output only. The Singapore products are nasal sprays, which are not a route suited to skin lymphoma, so the topical route would need separate consideration.
