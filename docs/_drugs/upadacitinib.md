## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, upadacitinib is a JAK1-preferential inhibitor of JAK-STAT signalling. Its efficacy in rheumatoid arthritis, an inflammatory autoimmune disease, has been established.

For this predicted indication, no mechanistic link could be identified. Colobomatous microphthalmia-rhizomelic dysplasia syndrome is an ultra-rare developmental malformation syndrome with no known involvement of JAK-STAT signalling. The high score (0.996) is a graph-based computational result and is not backed by any trial or publication. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis, not as evidence of benefit.

Other top-ranked predictions show the same pattern. Most are rare genetic or developmental conditions (for example brachydactyly-syndactyly syndrome and axial spondylometaphyseal dysplasia) or neurodegenerative conditions (several forms of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis) without a clear JAK-related mechanism. Plasma cell myeloma and indolent plasma cell myeloma have a theoretical rationale through IL-6/JAK/STAT3 signalling. However, no study has evaluated upadacitinib there, and JAK inhibitors carry malignancy and infection warnings.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is supported only by a model score. There are no clinical trials or publications, no plausible mechanistic link to JAK inhibition, and the evidence level is L5. The other nine top-ranked predictions have the same weakness. The only exception is plasma cell myeloma, where one retrieved paper concerns an adverse event of CAR-T therapy and does not assess upadacitinib.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**

- Mechanism of action data for upadacitinib

- Approved indication text for the Singapore licences
