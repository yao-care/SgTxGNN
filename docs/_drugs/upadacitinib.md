## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Based on known information, upadacitinib is a selective JAK1 inhibitor that acts on cytokine signaling. Its efficacy in inflammatory disease is established, but that effect is mediated through immune modulation.

The predicted disease is a rare developmental malformation syndrome with no recognized JAK/STAT-driven pathology. Immunosuppression would not be expected to alter a structural developmental defect.

The very high TxGNN score is probably a knowledge-graph artifact. Rare-disease nodes with few connections can receive inflated scores that do not reflect pharmacology. **In short, this prediction is not supported by biology.**
