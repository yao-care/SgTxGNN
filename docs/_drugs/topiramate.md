## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in this evidence pack (data gap DG002). Based on known pharmacology, topiramate acts through multiple complementary mechanisms: blockade of voltage-gated sodium channels, potentiation of GABA-A receptor activity, and antagonism of AMPA/kainate glutamate receptors. These are the same general classes of mechanism used by carbamazepine and oxcarbazepine — the current first-line drugs for trigeminal neuralgia — to dampen the abnormal, high-frequency ectopic firing of trigeminal nerve fibers that causes paroxysmal facial pain.

Trigeminal neuralgia and epilepsy share a common underlying pathophysiology of neuronal hyperexcitability and paroxysmal abnormal electrical discharge, just expressed in different anatomical circuits (facial sensory nerve vs. cortical/subcortical seizure networks). This is why sodium-channel-blocking and GABA-enhancing AEDs are already an established drug class for neuropathic facial pain, and it is the basis for topiramate's mechanistic plausibility as an alternative or adjunct when carbamazepine is not tolerated.

Unlike most of TxGNN's other top-ranked predictions for this drug (which appear to be driven by superficial semantic clustering around "epilepsy"/"seizure" terms with no real supporting evidence), the trigeminal neuralgia candidate is corroborated by a body of direct human clinical evidence spanning more than two decades, summarized below.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Topiramate for trigeminal neuralgia has a mechanistically sound basis (shared sodium-channel/GABA pathway with carbamazepine, the standard of care) and is supported by one RCT, two meta-analyses/systematic reviews, and several case series spanning 2000–2026 — sufficient to warrant structured clinical evaluation, but the trials are small (pilot studies, n=3–100) and none are large confirmatory Phase 3 trials, so this does not yet meet a "Go" bar.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- TFDA/HSA package insert data (warnings, contraindications) — currently a Blocking data gap (DG001)
- Detailed mechanism of action documentation from DrugBank (DG002)
- A larger, adequately powered RCT directly comparing topiramate to carbamazepine/oxcarbazepine in classical trigeminal neuralgia
- Local regulatory pathway assessment, since topiramate currently has zero product registrations in Singapore
