# Sacituzumab govitecan: From an Unspecified Approved Indication to Drug-Induced Osteoporosis

## One-Sentence Summary

Sacituzumab govitecan is a Trop-2-directed antibody-drug conjugate carrying a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload (SN-38), and it is marketed in Singapore as Trodelvy. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **drug-induced osteoporosis**, but **0 clinical trials** and **0 publications** currently support this direction, so the prediction rests on the model alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge of the drug class, sacituzumab govitecan is an antibody-drug conjugate. The antibody part targets Trop-2 on tumour cells and delivers SN-38, a topoisomerase I inhibitor, which is a cytotoxic mechanism.

This prediction is hard to justify mechanistically. No plausible link connects a cytotoxic, tumour-targeted agent to bone-protective effects. Cytotoxic chemotherapy and its supportive care, such as myelosuppression, gastrointestinal toxicity and steroid co-medication, are more likely to harm bone health than to treat bone loss. The high TxGNN score most likely reflects knowledge-graph artefacts rather than real biology.

The other top-ranked predictions are diabetic retinopathy (including severe non-proliferative disease) and several cataract types. They show the same pattern: all are Evidence Level L5, with no trials or literature, and no recognised mechanistic rationale.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is supported only by the model score (Evidence Level L5). There are no trials or publications, and no credible mechanism links a cytotoxic Trop-2-directed ADC to drug-induced osteoporosis. The systemic toxicity of this drug is also disproportionate to the proposed use, and chemotherapy tends to worsen bone health.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the HSA, and the approved indication text for the Singapore licence
- Mechanism of action data
- Any preclinical or clinical evidence linking the drug to bone metabolism
- A risk-benefit assessment against established osteoporosis therapies
