# Enfortumab vedotin: From Urothelial Cancer to Leprosy (Top TxGNN Prediction)

## One-Sentence Summary

Enfortumab vedotin is a Nectin-4-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) carrying an MMAE cytotoxic payload, marketed in Singapore as Padcev. The TxGNN model's top-ranked prediction is **leprosy**, but **no clinical trials and no publications** support this prediction. Among the ten predictions, only **HER2-positive breast carcinoma** has any supporting evidence (1 Phase 2 trial, 5 indirect publications), and that evidence is still indirect.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Enfortumab vedotin is an antibody-drug conjugate. The antibody binds Nectin-4, a cell-surface protein, and delivers the cytotoxic payload MMAE into the cell. Detailed mechanism-of-action data were not available in the data compiled for this report, so the description above is based on the drug class.

**Leprosy.** No mechanistic link has been established. The high score is a computational output only, and nothing in the data connects a Nectin-4-targeted cytotoxic ADC to a bacterial infection such as leprosy.

**Other top-ranked predictions.** The following have no plausible rationale and no supporting evidence:
- Multiple endocrine neoplasia
- Cerebral infarction
- HIV infectious disease
- Homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia
- Two veterinary diseases, infectious bovine rhinotracheitis and malignant catarrh. These share an identical score and are probably knowledge-graph artefacts.

The two infection-related predictions that have publications, cytomegalovirus infection and candidiasis, are linked only to adverse-event reports. One is a case of severe oesophagitis after enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab. The other is a pharmacovigilance (FAERS) analysis of ADC safety signals. These are safety observations, not evidence of therapeutic benefit.

**HER2-positive breast carcinoma** is the only biologically plausible prediction. Nectin-4 is expressed in a subset of breast cancers, notably triple-negative and some HER2-positive tumours. However, the available literature is mostly about bladder cancer or general ADC reviews. One paper on resistance to HER2-targeting ADCs is only indirectly relevant.

## Safety Considerations

**Drug Interactions:** The interaction screen returned 100 interactions in total. The first 20 listed include one **Major** interaction, clarithromycin, and several **Moderate** ones, mainly with antidiabetic drugs. Examples are metformin, pioglitazone, glimepiride, glipizide, glyburide, and the SGLT2 inhibitors canagliflozin, dapagliflozin and empagliflozin. Also listed are the DPP-4 inhibitors and GLP-1 agonists such as saxagliptin, linagliptin, exenatide and dulaglutide, along with aprepitant. The full list should be reviewed before any use.

For key warnings and contraindications, please refer to the package insert.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top-ranked prediction, leprosy, rests on a computational score alone. There are no trials or publications, and no mechanistic link to a Nectin-4-targeted cytotoxic ADC. Evidence for the other predictions is either absent or limited to adverse-event reports. HER2-positive breast carcinoma is the only plausible lead (L4, research question) but depends on unpublished cohort results.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Breast-cancer-specific results from the EV-202 trial (NCT04225117), and Nectin-4 expression data in HER2-positive tumours
- Mechanism-of-action data and the approved indication text from the Singapore package insert
- Package insert warnings and contraindications, which are required for safety screening
- Route and formulation compatibility assessment
- Review of the full interaction list, particularly the major interaction with clarithromycin
