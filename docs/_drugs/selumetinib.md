## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for selumetinib is not available in the data compiled for this report. Selumetinib is generally known as a MEK inhibitor acting on the RAS/MAPK pathway. This is background knowledge, not something confirmed by the compiled data.

Neurofibromatosis is a RAS/MAPK-driven disorder with prominent skin pigmentation features. Familial generalized lentiginosis is a pigmentary (lentiginous) skin disorder. The hypothesis is that it may share some pathway dysregulation, which would make MEK inhibition a plausible approach.

This link is speculative. It rests only on the model's network-based prediction, and no trial or literature evidence was found for this disease. The evidence level is therefore L5.
