# Indapamide: From Hypertension to Chronic Pulmonary Heart Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Indapamide is a thiazide-like diuretic used as an antihypertensive. The TxGNN model predicts it may help in **chronic pulmonary heart disease**, but **no clinical trials** and only **3 loosely related publications** exist, and none tests indapamide for this condition.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available for indapamide. Based on general pharmacology, thiazide-like diuretics reduce fluid volume and preload. In principle, this could ease congestion in a strained right heart.

The link between the original and new indications is indirect. Hypertension and chronic pulmonary heart disease are both cardiovascular conditions in which volume load matters. However, the retrieved literature does not test this. It consists of a general review on the progression from hypertension to heart failure, a review on cardiac insufficiency in COPD, and an overdose case report. The COPD article looked at a perindopril/indapamide combination, so indapamide's own contribution cannot be separated out.

## Safety Considerations

No structured warnings, contraindications, or drug interaction data are available. Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

The retrieved literature does report two safety signals:
- **Overdose:** prolonged hypotension, marked diuresis, and hypokalaemia (PMID 19623566). Diuretic-induced hypotension would be a concern in acute right-heart strain.
- **Ocular effects:** indapamide-associated choroidal effusion and acute angle-closure glaucoma (PMID 34736431).

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The high model score (92.97%) is not backed by any trial or by literature that tests indapamide for chronic pulmonary heart disease. The only supportive citation involves a combination product, and the mechanism and safety data are incomplete.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data for indapamide (currently missing from DrugBank).
- HSA package insert warnings and contraindications. This is a blocking gap for safety screening.
- Clinical data on indapamide alone in chronic pulmonary heart disease, for example a controlled study in COPD with right-heart involvement.
- Evaluation of the lower-ranked **chronic kidney disease** prediction, which has more supporting material: 3 trials (indirect), ADVANCE-related analyses of perindopril/indapamide, and older renal-impairment studies. It may be a better candidate for follow-up, though the effect of indapamide alone still cannot be separated from the ACE inhibitor combination.
