## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, alpha-tocopherol acetate is a vitamin E ester and a lipid-soluble antioxidant. It is marketed in Singapore only as a component of a multivitamin injection (CERNEVIT). Mechanistically, it may be applicable to drug-induced osteoporosis.

The only rationale on file is that antioxidant activity could plausibly limit oxidative stress in bone. No trial or publication was supplied to support this link. The relationship to the original indication cannot be assessed because no original indication is recorded.
