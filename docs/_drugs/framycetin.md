## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Framycetin is an aminoglycoside (the same compound as neomycin B). Aminoglycosides act against bacteria and have negligible systemic absorption when used topically or orally.

The only conceivable link to sclerosing cholangitis is a gut-microbiome hypothesis. A poorly absorbed antibiotic could change gut bacteria, and gut bacteria are thought to play a role in bile duct inflammation. No data were provided to support this idea, and no similarity to the original indication has been assessed. The high score may partly reflect a pattern in the knowledge graph rather than a real biological signal. It should be treated as a hypothesis only.
