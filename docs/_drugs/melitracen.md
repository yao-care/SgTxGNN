## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available. Melitracen belongs to the tricyclic antidepressant class. Drugs in this class generally have sedative, antihistaminic and anticholinergic properties, and these plausibly relate to sleep disturbance. Insomnia also commonly occurs together with anxiety and depression, which are the conditions this drug is typically used for.

The mechanistic link is therefore inferred from drug class rather than demonstrated for melitracen itself. The approved indication text in the Singapore record is empty, so the relationship between the original and new indication cannot be confirmed from the data compiled for this report.
