## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications suggested by the knowledge graph** (not taken from label text): hypercalcemia, nephrocalcinosis, nephrolithiasis, kidney disease, anuria, heart disease and coronary artery disease, myocardial infarction, hypertension, peptic ulcer and gastric ulcer, pancreatitis, liver disease, sarcoidosis, and others.
- **Local toxicity:** The linked literature documents corrosive gastric injury, including stenosis and penetration, after ingestion of calcium chloride.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found. Major interactions include:
  - **Ceftriaxone:** a calcium-containing solution can cause ceftriaxone-calcium precipitation.
  - **Digoxin:** calcium can increase the risk of cardiac toxicity.
  - Moderate interactions include calcium channel blockers (amlodipine, nifedipine, diltiazem, verapamil), thiazide diuretics (hydrochlorothiazide, chlorothiazide, chlorthalidone, metolazone), tetracyclines (doxycycline, tetracycline, minocycline, omadacycline) and topical vitamin D analogues (calcipotriol, calcitriol).

Please refer to the package insert for label warnings and contraindications, since these were not available for this report.
