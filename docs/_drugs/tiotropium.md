## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Tiotropium belongs to the long-acting muscarinic antagonist class. These drugs block acetylcholine receptors in the airways, relax airway smooth muscle and widen the airways.

If a respiratory malformation causes functional airway narrowing or obstruction, bronchodilation could in theory relieve some symptoms. This link is only indirect. Structural malformations are a developmental problem, and a bronchodilator does not correct the underlying anatomy. The high score most likely reflects the drug's proximity to other airway diseases in the model's network, not evidence in malformation patients.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interaction records were found. The main concern is additive anticholinergic effects (such as dry mouth, urinary retention, constipation and raised eye pressure) when tiotropium is combined with other antimuscarinic agents. The interacting drugs listed as Moderate include:
  - hyoscyamine, atropine, scopolamine and methscopolamine
  - glycopyrronium (including topical), clidinium, dicyclomine, mepenzolate, propantheline and trospium
  - eluxadoline, prucalopride and inhaled rapid-acting human insulin
- Cimetidine is listed as a Minor interaction.
- Interactions with calcitriol, pantoprazole, glimepiride, mesalazine and doxycycline are of unknown significance.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high (0.9995), but no trial or publication studies tiotropium in respiratory malformation, so the evidence level is L5. The available trials are in cystic fibrosis. A bronchodilator is unlikely to alter a fixed structural malformation, so any benefit would be limited to functional symptoms.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Clinical or observational data in patients with respiratory malformation, especially those with functional airway obstruction
- Detailed mechanism of action data and a clearer biological rationale for this specific condition
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator, which have not yet been reviewed
- A dosing and delivery assessment for the likely patient group, which may include young children
