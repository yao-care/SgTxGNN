## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, velaglucerase alfa is a recombinant glucocerebrosidase (GBA1) enzyme replacement therapy. It breaks down glucosylceramide that accumulates in Gaucher disease.

Steel syndrome is a skeletal dysplasia caused by COL27A1 variants, which is a collagen disorder. It has no known glucosylceramide accumulation pathway. No established mechanistic link therefore exists between the original and the predicted indication. The high score (0.970) is a graph-based prediction only. It is not supported by any trial or publication, and it should be treated as a hypothesis, not as evidence.
