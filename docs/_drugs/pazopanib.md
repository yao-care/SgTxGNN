# Pazopanib: From Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma to Xp11.2/TFE3-Translocation Renal Cell Carcinoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Pazopanib is an oral multi-target kinase inhibitor, marketed for advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC).
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **RCC associated with Xp11.2 translocations/TFE3 gene fusions**,
but there are currently **0 clinical trials** and **0 publications** for this specific subtype, so the prediction rests on the model alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Pazopanib blocks several receptor tyrosine kinases: VEGFR1-3, PDGFR-α/β and c-Kit. Blocking these receptors cuts off tumour blood-vessel growth. Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the drug record, so this description comes only from the evidence pack's mechanistic notes.

Both the approved and the predicted indication are renal cell carcinomas, and anti-angiogenic therapy could plausibly carry over to this subtype. However, Xp11.2/TFE3-translocation RCC is a biologically distinct subtype, and the pack contains no trial or literature evidence for pazopanib in it. The 99.63% score is a graph-based model output, not clinical proof.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
For this subtype there is no supporting trial or publication, so the prediction remains hypothesis-level (L5). The package-insert safety data is also missing, so safety screening cannot start.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The HSA package insert (warnings and contraindications), which is currently blocking
- Mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- A targeted literature and trial search for pazopanib in Xp11.2/TFE3-translocation RCC. Adult and paediatric data should be reviewed separately, because this subtype is biologically distinct from clear-cell RCC.

**Other predicted indications in the same pack have stronger support and may deserve priority.** These are unclassified RCC (L3), liposarcoma (L2), fibroblastic neoplasm (L2) and dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (L2). All four are rated "Proceed with Guardrails" in the pack, and separate reports would cover each.
