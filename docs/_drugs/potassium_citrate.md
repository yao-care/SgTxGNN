## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on general pharmacology, potassium citrate supplies potassium and citrate. Citrate is metabolized to bicarbonate, which raises urinary pH and citrate excretion. This is relevant to the potassium and acid-base conditions recorded in the knowledge graph.

No plausible mechanistic link was found between this action and familial visceral myopathy, a rare disorder affecting the smooth muscle of the gut and other visceral organs. The high score comes from the model's network-based inference, not from biological or clinical evidence. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
