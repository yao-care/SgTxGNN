# Methotrexate: From Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia and Rheumatoid Arthritis to Pulmonary Blastoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Methotrexate is an antifolate drug originally used for acute lymphoblastic leukemia, lymphomas, rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis and cutaneous T-cell lymphoma.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, methotrexate is an antifolate that inhibits dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR). This suppresses purine and pyrimidine synthesis and therefore DNA synthesis in rapidly dividing cells. Its efficacy in lymphoid malignancies (acute lymphoblastic leukemia, non-Hodgkin lymphoma) is established, and at lower doses it also acts as an anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory agent in rheumatoid arthritis and psoriasis.

For the top-ranked prediction, pulmonary blastoma, the link is only a general one: an antiproliferative drug acting on a proliferative lung malignancy. No study supports it. The predictions for lymphoid and sarcoma diseases (primary pulmonary lymphoma, Hodgkin lymphoma, rhabdomyosarcoma) are more plausible because methotrexate is already used in related cancers, and some historical studies exist. For small cell lung carcinoma, methotrexate was used in older combination regimens, but the data do not show a methotrexate-specific benefit.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindicated conditions (from the knowledge graph, not label text)**: liver failure and liver disease, kidney disease, gallbladder disease, thrombocytopenia, leukopenia, anemia (including macrocytic anemia), peptic or gastric ulcer disease, ulcerative colitis and inflammatory bowel disease, stomatitis, interstitial lung disease, pulmonary fibrosis (including postinflammatory), substance abuse/dependence, immune system disease, and pyoureter.
- **Drug interactions**: 100 interactions were found. The ones listed include **Major** interactions with rabeprazole, amoxicillin and acetylsalicylic acid. Moderate interactions include corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone, budesonide), tetracyclines (doxycycline, tetracycline), clarithromycin, amphotericin B, mesalazine, balsalazide, pioglitazone, acarbose, bupropion and chenodeoxycholic acid. Potassium citrate is Minor.
- **Methotrexate-associated lymphoproliferative disorders**: several case reports and cohort studies describe lymphoma-like disease arising in patients treated with methotrexate (mostly for rheumatoid arthritis). This matters when interpreting the lymphoma literature.

Please refer to the package insert for the official warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top-ranked prediction, pulmonary blastoma, rests on the model score alone (L5), with no trials or publications. Among the other predictions, Hodgkin lymphoma carries a nominal L1 grade only because methotrexate is mature in the lymphoma field. Its Phase 3 trials are in B-cell NHL or GVHD prophylaxis, not Hodgkin-specific. Rhabdomyosarcoma (L2) has only a historical Phase 2 study, and small cell lung carcinoma has no methotrexate-specific benefit. Primary pulmonary lymphoma and the VBM regimen in early-stage Hodgkin lymphoma are the most credible directions for further research, but none is ready to proceed.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator (a blocking data gap), and the approved indication text for each license
- Mechanism of action data
- For primary pulmonary lymphoma and Hodgkin lymphoma: a focused review of methotrexate-containing regimens (including VBM and high-dose methotrexate) against current standard therapy
- For rhabdomyosarcoma: confirmation of whether NCT00357084 concerns this disease, and a comparison with current standard regimens
- Route compatibility and similarity-to-original analyses, which are still pending
- A safety plan covering hepatic, renal, pulmonary and hematologic toxicity, and the interaction risks above
