## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on general pharmacology, mifepristone is a glucocorticoid receptor and progesterone receptor antagonist. Its efficacy in its original indication is established, but the data compiled for this report do not describe how it works.

A possible, purely speculative link is that blocking glucocorticoid receptor signalling could change inflammation on the ocular surface. Nothing in the compiled data supports this. Glucocorticoids are themselves used to reduce eye inflammation, so antagonising their receptor could just as plausibly worsen the condition. The high score (97.60%) is a computational prediction only and should not be read as evidence of efficacy.

Other conditions ranked by the model include rosacea (97.17%), otitis externa (96.65%), post-bacterial disorder (96.65%), postinfectious vasculitis (96.61%), infective urethral stricture (96.56%), post-infectious syndrome (96.54%), Chagas cardiomyopathy (96.45%), infection-related haemolytic uraemic syndrome (96.42%) and parasitic eyelid infestation (92.82%). None has any supporting trial or publication in the compiled data. Many are infection-related, for which mifepristone has no known anti-infective mechanism.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has no supporting clinical trials or publications (Evidence Level L5), and no mechanistic link is established. The knowledge graph flags several contraindications, including infectious disease, and mifepristone interacts strongly with corticosteroids, which are commonly used in eye and skin conditions.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data for mifepristone
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator (HSA)
- Preclinical or early clinical evidence in ocular surface disease
- A route-of-administration assessment (the only Singapore product is an oral tablet, whereas an eye condition would likely need a topical or ocular formulation)
- A safety review of ocular use, including the corticosteroid interaction and the knowledge-graph contraindications
