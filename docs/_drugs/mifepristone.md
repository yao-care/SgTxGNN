## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication signals from the knowledge graph** (these come from drug-disease relationship data, not from package insert text):
  - Adrenal conditions: adrenocortical insufficiency, Addison disease, adrenal insufficiency
  - Blood coagulation disease
  - Porphyrias: hepatic porphyria, variegate porphyria, porphyria cutanea tarda (familial and sporadic), inherited porphyria, inborn disorder of porphyrin metabolism, erythropoietic protoporphyria (including the autosomal form), acute intermittent porphyria
  - Infectious disease
  - Ectopic pregnancy
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. The sample reviewed included:
  - **Major**: hydrocortisone, triamcinolone, dexamethasone, beclomethasone dipropionate, betamethasone, budesonide, clarithromycin, dolasetron
  - **Moderate**: famotidine, pioglitazone, loperamide, bupropion, aprepitant, bisacodyl, budesonide (nasal), chlorpropamide, cimetidine, picosulfuric acid, polyethylene glycol (3350 with electrolytes), saxagliptin

  The many major interactions with corticosteroids are relevant because mifepristone blocks glucocorticoid receptors and could reduce their effect.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and further safety information.
