## Dasatinib: Ewing sarcoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Dasatinib is an oral multi-targeted kinase inhibitor that blocks BCR-ABL and Src-family kinases.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **Ewing sarcoma**, supported by **2 clinical trials** (one small terminated trial and one mixed-sarcoma phase 2 study) and **10 retrieved publications**, mostly laboratory studies and reviews.
The evidence level is **L3**: there is no randomized trial in Ewing sarcoma.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on what is known, dasatinib is a kinase inhibitor that acts on BCR-ABL, Src-family kinases, c-KIT and PDGFR.

Laboratory studies show that Src signalling drives invadopodia formation, migration and invasion in Ewing sarcoma cell lines, and that dasatinib reduces migration, invasion and proliferation in sarcoma and Ewing sarcoma cell lines (PMIDs 18202781, 27566104, 31521948, 17363602). Reviews also describe Src/FAK signalling as a plausible target in this tumour (PMIDs 35655525, 26170970).

The clinical picture is less encouraging. A review notes that single-agent dasatinib failed in the Ewing sarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma subtypes of a phase 2 sarcoma study. The mechanistic rationale is therefore consistent, but clinical efficacy in Ewing sarcoma has not been shown.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Preclinical data are consistent, and a completed phase 2 sarcoma trial and a small case series involved Ewing sarcoma. However, single-agent dasatinib did not show benefit in the Ewing sarcoma subtype, and there is no randomized trial. The TxGNN score is high (0.9990), but it does not replace clinical evidence.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Subtype-specific results from NCT00464620 for the Ewing sarcoma cohort
- Evaluation of combination approaches, such as the RIST regimen, in a prospective study
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- A cardiac, haematological and drug-interaction monitoring plan, particularly for patients on acid-reducing drugs
