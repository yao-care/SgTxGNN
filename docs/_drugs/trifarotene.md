## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for trifarotene. Based on the information included in this evidence pack, trifarotene acts as a topical RAR-γ (retinoic acid receptor gamma) agonist, and comparator predictions (e.g. pyoderma gangrenosum-acne syndrome, sebaceous gland demodicidosis) suggest its established use involves acne and follicular/sebaceous unit disorders.

For the top-ranked candidate — elevated plasma zinc — the evidence pack itself states there is **no known mechanism** linking RAR-γ agonism to plasma zinc dysregulation. This association appears to be a statistical artifact of the knowledge graph rather than a biologically grounded hypothesis, and no corroborating mechanistic, preclinical, or clinical data are available.

Given trifarotene's known role as a topical dermatologic agent, an intravascular or systemic metabolic indication such as elevated plasma zinc is not mechanistically coherent based on route of administration or pharmacology as currently understood. This candidate should be treated as exploratory signal only, not a validated repurposing hypothesis.
