## Safety Considerations

The package insert warnings and contraindications were not available in the compiled data, so please refer to the package insert for the official safety information.

Supplementary information from other sources:

- **Possible disease-related contraindications (knowledge-graph data, not label text):** liver failure, kidney disease, diabetes mellitus and diabetic ketoacidosis, lung disease, leukopenia, uveitis and other eye inflammation, macular oedema (retinal and cystoid), atrioventricular block, and sick sinus syndrome. These should be confirmed against the official label.
- **Drug interactions:** 100 interactions were identified; the 20 reviewed include these.
  - **Major:** systemic and ophthalmic corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, prednisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, triamcinolone), clarithromycin, levofloxacin, ondansetron, dolasetron, palonosetron, and cisapride.
  - **Moderate:** famotidine, ranitidine, cimetidine, nizatidine, loperamide, and naltrexone.
  - **Minor:** metronidazole.

Fingolimod lowers circulating lymphocytes, which is a concern for any population with immune or haematological vulnerability.
