## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication-related conditions (from a knowledge graph, not label text)**: hypoglycaemia and pancreatitis.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. The sample reviewed was mostly Moderate, with a few Minor. Examples of Moderate interactions include:
  - sympathomimetics and bronchodilators (phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine, epinephrine, salbutamol, formoterol)
  - corticosteroids (hydrocortisone)
  - hormonal agents (estradiol, levonorgestrel, norethisterone, desogestrel)
  - other drugs (hydrochlorothiazide, benazepril, acetazolamide, alpelisib, apalutamide, amobarbital, ethanol, abametapir topical)
  - Minor examples are topical hydrocortisone and topical alclometasone.

For the full warnings and contraindications, please refer to the package insert.
