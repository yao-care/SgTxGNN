## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, vaborbactam is a beta-lactamase inhibitor that protects meropenem from serine carbapenemases such as KPC. It is an antibacterial adjunct and has no known activity on joint cartilage or on inflammatory or degenerative pathways.

No plausible mechanistic link to osteoarthritis was identified. The high TxGNN score reflects proximity in a knowledge graph, not biological or clinical evidence. The other top predictions (osteoarthritis susceptibility, rheumatoid arthritis, gout, and several rare skeletal dysplasias) show the same pattern: all are model predictions with no supporting trials or literature, and they appear to arise from shared graph neighbours among musculoskeletal disease nodes.

This prediction should be treated as a computational hypothesis only.
