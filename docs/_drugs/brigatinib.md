## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for brigatinib: "Brigatinib is a tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) with in vitro activity at clinically achievable concentrations against multiple kinases including ALK, ROS1, insulin-like growth factor-1 receptor (IGF-1R), and FLT-3 as well as EGFR deletion and point mutations. Brigatinib inhibited autophosphorylation of ALK and ALK-mediated phosphorylation of the downstream signaling proteins STAT3, AKT, ERK1/2, and S6 in in vitro and in vivo assays." ([DailyMed: Alunbrig (brigatinib) prescribing information, Takeda Pharmaceuticals America, Inc.](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=0fe9ff20-d402-41f3-bc1e-7002ea7007db), label version 2026-01-12; accessed 2026-11-19.) Brigatinib is a kinase inhibitor that acts on ALK, and its clinical development has centred on ALK-driven cancers.

No mechanistic link between brigatinib and gingival fibromatosis has been identified. The high TxGNN score (0.9989) reflects a model-based prediction only. It does not show that the drug acts on the biology of this condition. No trials or publications were found that examine brigatinib in gingival fibromatosis, so the prediction should be treated as a hypothesis that has not been tested.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**
