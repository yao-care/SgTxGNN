# Bumetanide: From Edema (Loop Diuretic Use) to Acute Pulmonary Heart Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Bumetanide is a potent loop diuretic, used for edema associated with heart failure, liver disease and kidney disease.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **acute pulmonary heart disease (acute cor pulmonale)**.
Support is weak: **3 clinical trials** and **5 publications** are linked, but none study acute cor pulmonale directly. The trials are all heart failure studies, one of them withdrawn with no participants.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the database. From general pharmacology, bumetanide blocks the NKCC2 transporter in the thick ascending limb of the loop of Henle. This produces rapid sodium and water loss and dilation of the veins, which lowers preload and right-sided congestion.

Acute cor pulmonale is right heart strain caused by a sudden rise in pulmonary pressure, often with fluid overload. The reasoning is that bumetanide's proven effect on congestion in heart failure might carry over to this setting. Existing data come from **left-sided** acute or decompensated heart failure, not from acute cor pulmonale. This is therefore an indirect extrapolation, not a tested use.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information. No interaction records were found in the database.

- **Specific concern for this indication**: Over-diuresis can reduce right ventricular preload. It can also cause metabolic alkalosis, which may worsen hypercapnia.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is high, but no trial or publication tests bumetanide in acute cor pulmonale. The linked evidence is indirect, from left heart failure. The only trial of bumetanide's effect on pulmonary artery pressure was withdrawn with no participants.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The HSA package insert, to obtain approved indications, warnings and contraindications
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Studies, or at least right heart hemodynamic data, in acute cor pulmonale patients
- A safety assessment of over-diuresis and acid-base effects in right heart strain

Among this drug's other predicted indications, **chronic kidney disease** has the strongest support. A completed Phase 2 trial (HEBE-CKD, NCT03923933) and a related publication (PMID 36127661) address bumetanide plus chlorthalidone in advanced CKD. It may be a better candidate to prioritise, though volume overload in renal disease is already close to the labeled edema use.
