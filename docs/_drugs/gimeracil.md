# Gimeracil: From Gastric Cancer to Colonic Neoplasm

## One-Sentence Summary

Gimeracil is a component of the S-1 combination (tegafur/gimeracil/oteracil), a fluoropyrimidine regimen used for gastric cancer.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Colonic Neoplasm**, with **8 clinical trials** (3 Phase 3 RCTs, 2 of them completed) and **15 publications** currently supporting this direction.
Note that all of this evidence concerns the S-1 combination, not gimeracil alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Gimeracil is a reversible inhibitor of dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD), the enzyme that breaks down 5-fluorouracil (5-FU). In S-1, gimeracil slows 5-FU breakdown and prolongs systemic 5-FU exposure. This is the basis of the fluoropyrimidine activity seen in colorectal cancer. Gimeracil has no antitumour activity on its own.

Gastric cancer and colorectal cancer are both gastrointestinal adenocarcinomas that respond to fluoropyrimidines. S-1 regimens have been tested in colorectal cancer in randomized trials, both as adjuvant treatment and in metastatic disease. Colorectal cancer is also likely an approved use of S-1 in some jurisdictions, but this should be confirmed locally.

## Safety Considerations

Package insert warnings, contraindications and drug interaction data were not available in the source data. Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

Adverse events reported in the retrieved literature on S-1 include:
- Haematological toxicity (with irinotecan)
- Hand-foot syndrome and erythroderma with mucosal involvement
- Hypertriglyceridemia
- Possible cardiac effects, which one terminated Phase 2 study was designed to examine

As with other fluoropyrimidines, DPD/DPYD-related toxicity risk should be assessed.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Two completed Phase 3 RCTs (NCT00660894, NCT01918852) plus a large adjuvant Phase 3 trial of unknown status (NCT03448549) support S-1 regimens in colorectal cancer, giving evidence level L1. However, this evidence is for the three-component S-1 product, not gimeracil alone, and no published RCT results were retrieved.

Other predicted indications are much weaker. Rectosigmoid junction neoplasm and cardia cancer are research questions only, with indirect or case-report-level evidence. The remaining predictions (benign lesions, rare tumours, gastric lymphoma) have no supporting evidence and should be held.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert warnings and contraindications, which are currently missing and block safety screening
- Confirmation of the approved indications of TS-ONE in Singapore, since the registration records show no indication text, and of whether colorectal cancer is already covered
- Published efficacy and safety results from NCT00660894 and NCT03448549
- A DPD/DPYD testing and toxicity monitoring plan
- Confirmation that the trials included colon and rectosigmoid adenocarcinoma before extrapolating to those sites
