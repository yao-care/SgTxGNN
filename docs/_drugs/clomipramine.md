# Clomipramine: From Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder to Anxiety Disorder

## One-Sentence Summary

Clomipramine is a tricyclic antidepressant, originally used to treat obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD).
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **anxiety disorder**,
with **8 clinical trials** (mostly in OCD) and **20 publications** currently supporting this direction. Direct anxiety-disorder trial evidence is limited, so much of the support is inferred from OCD and panic disorder studies.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, clomipramine is a potent serotonin reuptake inhibitor. Its metabolite, desmethylclomipramine, adds noradrenergic activity. Together these modulate the limbic and prefrontal circuits involved in anxiety-spectrum conditions.

OCD was historically classified as an anxiety disorder, and the two conditions share symptoms and respond to similar treatments. The literature supports clomipramine's efficacy in panic disorder, including network meta-analyses and treatment guidelines. Several older controlled studies also point to benefit in panic disorder with agoraphobia.

Most of the registered trials are OCD-focused (the original indication), so evidence for pure anxiety disorders is partly inferred from OCD and panic literature.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication signals (from knowledge-graph associations, not label text):** epilepsy, manic bipolar disorder, liver disease, gallbladder disease, substance abuse or dependence, malignant hyperthermia, pheochromocytoma and paraganglioma, glaucoma, hyperthyroidism (including Graves disease and thyrotoxicosis), neuroblastoma, and several cancers (duodenum, small intestine, heart, adrenal gland).
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were recorded. Major-level examples include epinephrine, bupropion, lorcaserin, potassium citrate and cisapride. Moderate-level examples include famotidine, cimetidine, clarithromycin, morphine, aspirin and several anticholinergic agents (atropine, hyoscyamine, glycopyrronium).

Please refer to the package insert for full warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Clomipramine has long-standing evidence in OCD and panic disorder, supported by network meta-analyses and guidelines, and it is already marketed in Singapore. However, no trial directly tests it in a broad anxiety disorder population, and tolerability issues (anticholinergic effects, cardiac effects, seizure risk) and many drug interactions require careful management.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Singapore package insert warnings and approved indication text
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Direct randomised evidence in anxiety disorders (for example panic disorder or agoraphobia)
- A safety monitoring plan covering cardiac risk, seizure history and interacting medicines
