# Clomipramine: From Depression and OCD to Anxiety Disorder

## One-Sentence Summary

Clomipramine is a tricyclic antidepressant, with literature describing established use in depressive illness and obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD). The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Anxiety Disorder**. The evidence pack lists **18 clinical trial records** and **20 publications**. Most trials involve OCD rather than classic anxiety disorders, but several placebo-controlled panic disorder and agoraphobia studies support the direction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available from DrugBank in this pack. Based on the analysis notes, clomipramine is a strong serotonin reuptake inhibitor with additional noradrenergic activity. Its active metabolite, desmethylclomipramine, adds to the noradrenergic effect.

Serotonergic modulation is the accepted mechanism in OCD, panic disorder and phobic anxiety. Serotonin reuptake inhibition is thought to reduce panic attacks and anticipatory anxiety. Published work also examined the relationship between serum levels of clomipramine and desmethylclomipramine and clinical response in panic disorder (PMID 10221358).

The literature shows clomipramine was long considered a standard treatment for panic disorder in Europe. It was one of the first drugs approved for it, alongside alprazolam (PMID 19698677, PMID 10361962). This supports the model's prediction, although the evidence is mostly from the 1980s and 1990s.

The literature returned for this drug also reports these signals:

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Several placebo-controlled trials in panic disorder and agoraphobia support clomipramine, and the serotonergic mechanism is plausible. The evidence is mostly old, small, and not from registered trials. The registered clomipramine trial is OCD-specific, Phase 2 and of unknown status. Clomipramine is already marketed in Singapore, so a formal new-indication assessment is feasible.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert warnings, contraindications and approved indications, which are blocking for safety screening
- DrugBank mechanism-of-action data
- Drug-interaction data, which returned no results; CYP2D6/CYP2C19 metabolism and serotonergic co-medications are the priority checks
- A clear choice of anxiety subtype (panic disorder/agoraphobia vs generalised anxiety), since the evidence is concentrated in panic disorder and agoraphobia
- A review of current guidelines comparing clomipramine with SSRIs and SNRIs, given the tolerability profile of tricyclics
- A safety monitoring plan for older patients and those with cardiac disease or seizure history

Other predictions for this drug include major depressive disorder (L1, Proceed with Guardrails) and agoraphobia (L2, Proceed with Guardrails). They may be worth evaluating separately.
