## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for ascorbic acid are not available in the data compiled for this report. In general, ascorbic acid acts as an antioxidant and as a cofactor for hydroxylase enzymes involved in collagen formation.

No mechanistic rationale links these properties to non-syndromic esophageal malformation. This is a congenital structural condition, and there is no plausible way a vitamin would correct a developmental anatomical defect. The high score of 0.9996 is a model output and should not be read as evidence of benefit.
