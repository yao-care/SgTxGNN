## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled records. Siltuximab is a chimeric monoclonal antibody that targets IL-6. Its efficacy in Castleman disease, a condition driven by dysregulated IL-6 production, has been established. Mechanistically, it could be applicable to other conditions in which IL-6 signaling plays a role.

For extracutaneous mastocytoma, the link is weak. IL-6 signaling has been implicated in mast cell biology, but no clinical or literature support was found for siltuximab in this indication. The prediction should be regarded as a hypothesis generated by the model, not as an evidence-based lead.
