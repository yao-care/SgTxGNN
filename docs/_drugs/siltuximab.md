## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Based on known information, siltuximab neutralizes IL-6, and its efficacy in iMCD, a disease driven by dysregulated IL-6, has been established. Mechanistically, it may be applicable to other conditions where IL-6 is elevated.

IL-6 is reported to be elevated in mast cell disorders, and that is the only rationale offered for this prediction. No data directly link IL-6 blockade to extracutaneous mastocytoma, and the similarity to the original indication has not been assessed. The prediction should therefore be read as a hypothesis generated by the model, not as supported evidence.
