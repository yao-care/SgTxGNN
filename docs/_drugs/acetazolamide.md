## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Acetazolamide is known as a carbonic anhydrase inhibitor, a class that alters acid–base balance and ion handling in tissues.

One plausible, but speculative, link is that changes in muscle pH and ion handling could influence the heat and metabolic stress seen in exercise-induced malignant hyperthermia. No study retrieved for this report tests or supports that idea. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis generated by the model, not as evidence of benefit.
