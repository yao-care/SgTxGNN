## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, cyclizine blocks histamine H1 receptors and also has anticholinergic activity, and its central H1 and muscarinic blockade underlies its antiemetic use.

Allergic urticaria is a histamine-mediated condition, and H1 blockade is the established mechanism for symptom relief. That makes the predicted link biologically plausible. However, the high score (0.9998) is a computational prediction only. No cyclizine-specific study in urticaria was supplied.

Current practice generally favours second-generation antihistamines for urticaria because they are better tolerated and less sedating than first-generation drugs such as cyclizine. This limits the practical appeal of cyclizine for this use even if it is pharmacologically active.
