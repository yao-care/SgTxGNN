# Dacomitinib: From Its Licensed Oncology Use to Rheumatoid Arthritis

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the compiled data. Based on known information, dacomitinib is an irreversible pan-HER/EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor, a class used in oncology. Mechanistically, EGFR signalling has been implicated in the proliferation of synovial fibroblasts, which is the only rationale linking it to rheumatoid arthritis.

This link is speculative. There is no clinical or published evidence for dacomitinib in rheumatoid arthritis. The known safety profile of the drug (rash, diarrhoea, interstitial lung disease) is also a concern for a chronic, non-oncology condition that requires long-term treatment.

**Other predictions, for context.** The model's other top predictions are also weakly supported:
- **Pulmonary hypertension (score 96.51%, evidence level L4):** The only candidate with published support. A 2019 preclinical study found that dacomitinib attenuated pulmonary vascular remodelling and pulmonary hypertension in rats. It is a research question rather than a clinical candidate, and pulmonary toxicity from EGFR inhibitors needs careful consideration.
- **Multiple endocrine neoplasia (score 96.18%, L4):** One terminated Phase 1 platform trial with 2 participants provides no usable signal.
- **Homozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia, brachydactyly-syndactyly syndrome, nephrogenic syndrome of inappropriate antidiuresis, colobomatous microphthalmia-rhizomelic dysplasia syndrome, kyphoscoliotic heart disease, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis and leprosy:** Prediction only, with no plausible mechanistic link identified.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The rheumatoid arthritis prediction rests on a model score alone (L5), with no trials or literature. The drug's toxicity profile is difficult to justify for a chronic non-oncology indication.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Singapore package insert (warnings, contraindications and approved indications), which is currently a blocking gap for safety screening
- Mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Preclinical evidence of dacomitinib in rheumatoid arthritis models (for example, synovial fibroblast or arthritis animal models)
- A risk-benefit assessment of long-term EGFR inhibition in a non-oncology population
- Separately, pulmonary hypertension could be followed up as a research question, given the 2019 preclinical study (PMID 30753867), after its pulmonary safety is evaluated
