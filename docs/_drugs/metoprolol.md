## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Based on known information, metoprolol is a beta-1 selective blocker. Beta-1 blockade lowers renin release and heart rate, which is a generic antihypertensive rationale. Renin release is relevant here because renovascular hypertension is driven largely by the renin-angiotensin system.

The approved indication text for the Singapore licences is empty in the data, so the original-to-new indication relationship could not be assessed directly. The very high score is a model output. It is not backed by any drug-specific evidence.

The two retrieved papers do not concern metoprolol in this condition. One is a case commentary on hypertensive optic neuropathy caused by renal artery stenosis. The other is a diagnostic study on chromogranin A in pheochromocytoma. The related entry "malignant hypertensive renal disease" has an identical score, which suggests a shared graph neighbourhood rather than independent signal.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has no supporting trials and no drug-specific literature. It remains at model-prediction level (L5). Safety screening also cannot proceed because the package insert data are missing.

Other predicted indications in the pack have more evidence but still do not amount to a strong repurposing case:
- **Chronic pulmonary heart disease** is at L4. Its evidence concerns metoprolol in COPD, including the terminated Phase 3 BLOCK-COPD trial (NCT02587351), which showed no reduction in exacerbations and a signal toward more hospitalised exacerbations. It is a tolerability question, not an efficacy signal.
- **Posterolateral and septal myocardial infarction** are likely subtypes of established post-MI use rather than true repurposing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Approved indication text for the Singapore licences, to confirm which indications are already covered
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Any direct metoprolol evidence in renovascular or malignant hypertension. Without it, this candidate should not advance.
