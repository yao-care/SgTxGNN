# Lutropin alfa: From Infertility Treatment to Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome

## One-Sentence Summary

Lutropin alfa is a recombinant human luteinizing hormone (LH) used in fertility treatment. In Singapore it is marketed alone (Luveris) and in combination with follitropin alfa (Pergoveris).
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome**, but there are currently **0 clinical trials** and **0 publications** supporting this direction. The prediction rests on the computational score alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, lutropin alfa is a recombinant human LH that acts on LH/choriogonadotropin receptors in gonadal tissue. Its established role is in ovarian stimulation, not cardiovascular or autonomic regulation.

No established mechanistic link to postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS) has been identified. The high TxGNN score (97.04%) is a knowledge-graph prediction only, and the model's rank for this pair (21,133) is not strong. Because the mechanism of action data is missing, no pathway-level rationale can be assessed.

The other top-ranked predictions are similarly unsupported: peptic esophagitis (97.02%), trichotillomania (96.31%), Raynaud disease (95.65%), duodenal ulcer (95.58%), Tourette syndrome (95.18%), esophageal disease, sinoatrial block, multiple endocrine neoplasia and duodenogastric reflux. None has trials or literature, and no biologically plausible link to LH receptor agonism. The scores likely reflect proximity in the knowledge graph rather than biology.
