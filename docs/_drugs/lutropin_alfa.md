# Lutropin alfa: From Infertility Treatment to Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome

## One-Sentence Summary

Lutropin alfa is a recombinant luteinizing hormone (LH), and its Singapore-registered products (Luveris, Pergoveris) are fertility medicines.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS)**, but **0 clinical trials** and **0 publications** currently support this direction, so it is a computational prediction only.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available in the record. Lutropin alfa is a recombinant LH that acts on the LH/hCG receptor, a G-protein-coupled receptor found mainly in gonadal tissue. Its established role is supporting follicle development in reproductive treatment.

No clear mechanistic link to POTS was identified. POTS is a disorder of autonomic regulation and blood-pressure and heart-rate control on standing. A connection through sex-hormone effects on vascular tone is possible in principle, but it is speculative. There is no evidence that LH-receptor agonism affects orthostatic intolerance.

The high TxGNN score (97.04%) most likely reflects proximity in the knowledge graph rather than biology. The score should be read as a prompt for further review, not as evidence of efficacy.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has no supporting trials or publications (L5), and no plausible mechanism links LH-receptor signalling to POTS. The registration data also lack a stated original indication, warnings and contraindications, which limits any safety screening.

The other nine top-ranked predictions were also reviewed:
- All are computational only and rated Hold.
- Their diseases are peptic esophagitis, trichotillomania, Raynaud disease, duodenal ulcer, Tourette syndrome, esophageal disease, sinoatrial block, multiple endocrine neoplasia and duodenogastric reflux.
- Only one linked trial exists (NCT01081626, for multiple endocrine neoplasia). It is a Phase 4 study of recombinant FSH in ovulation induction and is very likely a matching artifact, not evidence for this indication.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The HSA package insert, to obtain approved indications, warnings and contraindications
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- A literature search for LH or sex-hormone involvement in POTS, to test whether any biological rationale exists
- A check of the conditions field for NCT01081626 on ClinicalTrials.gov, if the multiple endocrine neoplasia candidate is revisited
- A route and dosing compatibility assessment, which is still pending
