# Brexpiprazole: From Schizophrenia and Adjunctive Depression Treatment to Dysthymic Disorder

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the database. From general pharmacology, brexpiprazole is a partial agonist at dopamine D2/D3 and serotonin 5-HT1A receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2A receptors. This profile underlies its use as an add-on to antidepressants in major depressive disorder, where a Phase 3 randomised trial exists (PMID 26301701).

Dysthymic disorder is a chronic, lower-grade form of depression. It is closely related to major depression, so a link is plausible in principle. However, the retrieved data contain no trials or publications on brexpiprazole in dysthymia or persistent depressive disorder. The existing depression evidence is for major depressive disorder with inadequate response to antidepressants, which is a different population.

The link is therefore plausible but unverified. The high score reflects the model's graph-based association, not clinical proof.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The dysthymic disorder prediction has a high model score but no supporting trials or literature (L5). Safety data from the package insert are missing, which blocks safety screening.

Two other predictions for this drug, neurotic depression and melancholia, carry L1 evidence. That evidence is for adjunctive use in major depressive disorder, which is largely an existing-label use rather than new repurposing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert (warnings, contraindications, approved indications). This is the blocking gap.
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank.
- A targeted literature and trial-registry search for brexpiprazole in persistent depressive disorder or dysthymia.
- If depression-related use is pursued, guardrails of adjunctive use only, and monitoring for akathisia, weight gain and metabolic effects. Boxed warnings for suicidality and dementia-related mortality also need confirming against the package insert.
