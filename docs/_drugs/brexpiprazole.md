# Brexpiprazole: From Schizophrenia and Depressive Disorders to Neurotic Depression

## One-Sentence Summary

Brexpiprazole is an atypical antipsychotic originally used for schizophrenia and as an add-on treatment in major depressive disorder and anxiety disorder.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Published reviews describe brexpiprazole as a serotonin-dopamine activity modulator. It is a partial agonist at 5-HT1A and D2 receptors and a potent antagonist at 5-HT2A and noradrenergic α1B/α2C receptors.

Dysthymic disorder is a chronic, milder form of depression closely related to major depressive disorder, where brexpiprazole is already used as an add-on to antidepressants. This makes the prediction biologically coherent. However, the model score is the only support: no trials or publications were found specifically for dysthymic disorder.

Other predictions fall into two groups:
- **Plausible, depression/anxiety spectrum:** agoraphobia, neurotic disorder, neurotic depression and melancholia. Their high scores likely reflect overlap with the approved indications.
- **No evident mechanistic link:** benign paroxysmal torticollis of infancy, Keppen-Lubinsky syndrome, Ohdo syndrome, inherited retinal dystrophy and congenital isolated ACTH deficiency. These appear to be network-level associations only. For benign paroxysmal torticollis, antipsychotic-related dystonia and extrapyramidal effects are an added safety concern.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found in the interaction database. Examples of those rated **Major**: bupropion, aprepitant, morphine, lorcaserin, cimetidine, clarithromycin and clotrimazole. Examples rated **Moderate** include antidiabetic drugs (metformin, pioglitazone, acarbose, alogliptin, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin), anticholinergics (atropine, hyoscyamine, clidinium), epinephrine and loperamide.
- **Other considerations:** Antipsychotics can cause dystonia and extrapyramidal effects, which is relevant to the movement-disorder prediction (benign paroxysmal torticollis of infancy).

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top prediction, dysthymic disorder, rests on the model score alone (L5), with no trials or publications. Neurotic depression and melancholia reach L3 only through indirect MDD evidence, including one phase 3 RCT of adjunctive brexpiprazole in MDD, and are best treated as research questions rather than repurposing candidates.
