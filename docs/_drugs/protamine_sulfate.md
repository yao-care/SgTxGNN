## Safety Considerations

- **Hypersensitivity and anaphylaxis**: The literature compiled for this report consistently describes serious, sometimes fatal, anaphylactic or anaphylactoid reactions to protamine. Reported risk factors include prior exposure to protamine-containing insulin (NPH), fish allergy, vasectomy, and previous protamine exposure. Reactions can also occur without identifiable risk factors.
- **Mast cell activation**: Protamine is a polycationic peptide that can trigger mast cell degranulation and histamine release.

For other safety information, including formal warnings, contraindications and drug interactions, please refer to the package insert.
