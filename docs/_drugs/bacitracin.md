## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Bacitracin is known to inhibit bacterial cell-wall synthesis by binding undecaprenyl pyrophosphate. It has Gram-positive activity and is used topically, including on the eye.

The mechanistic fit is weak. Punctate epithelial keratoconjunctivitis is often viral, toxic or dry-eye related rather than a primary bacterial infection, so an antibacterial rationale is hard to justify. The link currently rests only on a knowledge-graph score, with no trial or literature support.
