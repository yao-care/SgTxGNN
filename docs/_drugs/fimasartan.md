## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, fimasartan is an angiotensin II type 1 receptor (AT1R) blocker. Its use in hypertension is recorded in the knowledge graph, and mechanistically it may be applicable to pulmonary vascular disease.

The renin-angiotensin system is implicated in pulmonary vascular remodelling and hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction, so blocking AT1R gives a plausible but unproven rationale. The high TxGNN score (0.992) is a computational prediction only. It is likely driven by shared hypertension and vascular pathway annotations among AT1R blockers.

The only linked publication studies myocardial ischaemia/reperfusion injury in an animal model, not pulmonary hypertension, so it offers no direct support. There is also a safety concern: systemic vasodilation and low blood pressure could worsen ventilation-perfusion matching in hypoxic lung disease.

Two other predictions were generated with the same score level: pulmonary hypertension with unclear multifactorial mechanism (0.9920) and malignant hypertensive renal disease (0.9916). Neither has any trials or literature. The renal prediction is the most mechanistically coherent, since RAS inhibition is a standard part of managing hypertensive kidney disease. However, malignant hypertension is an acute emergency usually managed with IV agents, and fimasartan already overlaps with the hypertension use, so it is not a true repurposing.
