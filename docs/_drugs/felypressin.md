## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, felypressin is a vasopressin analogue that mainly acts as a V1a receptor agonist, producing vasoconstriction. It is used with dental local anaesthetics to prolong anaesthesia and reduce local bleeding.

The link to drug-induced osteoporosis is weak. Some preclinical reports describe vasopressin receptor signalling in bone cells, but this is indirect and has not been validated for felypressin. Bone loss and local vasoconstriction are unrelated therapeutic areas, so the very high score (99.80%) most likely reflects proximity in the knowledge graph rather than established biology.
