## Zanubrutinib: From Mantle Cell Lymphoma to Myeloid Leukemia
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Zanubrutinib is an oral BTK inhibitor, originally used for B-cell cancers such as mantle cell lymphoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **myeloid leukemia**, but only **1 clinical trial** (a Phase 1 study in which zanubrutinib is only a possible combination partner) and no directly relevant publications support this direction.
The high model score is therefore not yet backed by clinical data.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, zanubrutinib is a Bruton tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitor. Its efficacy in B-cell malignancies is well documented, and mechanistically it may be applicable to myeloid leukemia.

BTK is a key mediator of B-cell receptor signaling in B-cell cancers. It is also expressed in myeloid-lineage cells and has been implicated in signaling in acute and chronic myeloid leukemia. This is the main biological basis for the prediction.

However, the publications compiled for this report are almost entirely about B-cell malignancies (CLL/SLL, mantle cell lymphoma, Waldenström's macroglobulinemia), not myeloid leukemia. The only trial retrieved tests a different drug (PRT2527, a CDK9 inhibitor), and zanubrutinib appears there only as a possible combination partner in B-cell cohorts. The high TxGNN score is not backed by direct clinical data.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is high, but no clinical study has tested zanubrutinib in myeloid leukemia. The only trial retrieved is a Phase 1 study of a different drug, and the supporting literature concerns B-cell malignancies. Zanubrutinib is already marketed in Singapore, but the evidence for this new indication is limited to a mechanistic hypothesis.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Preclinical or early clinical data on zanubrutinib in myeloid leukemia (AML or CML)
- Detailed mechanism of action data (MOA)
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore HSA
- A review of the major drug interactions (CYP3A-related agents, antiplatelet drugs) for the target patient population
- Confirmation of whether the NCT05665530 cohorts include any myeloid leukemia patients
