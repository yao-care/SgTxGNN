## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were recorded. The major-level ones in the displayed results are **deferiprone** and **samarium (153Sm) lexidronam**. Moderate-level interactions include:
  - corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, betamethasone, budesonide, dexamethasone, prednisolone, prednisone, triamcinolone)
  - acetylsalicylic acid
  - anticoagulants (heparin, argatroban, lepirudin)
  - radiopharmaceuticals and radioimmunotherapies (ibritumomab tiuxetan, tositumomab, iobenguane I-131, strontium-89)
  - palifermin
- **Literature signals:** Alemtuzumab carries a documented risk of infection and immunosuppression (PMID 15590388). EBV-related lymphoproliferative complications have been reported (PMID 18502831).

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.
