## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank is not available in the supplied data. From general pharmacology, trimetazidine inhibits 3-ketoacyl-CoA thiolase. This partly shifts cardiac and cellular metabolism from fatty acid oxidation toward glucose oxidation, which may help cells under energetic stress.

This could plausibly influence cellular energetics. However, a primary defect in oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS) caused by nuclear DNA anomalies is a genetic problem of the respiratory chain. No evidence in the supplied data shows that shifting substrate use corrects such a defect.

The link is therefore speculative. The high score reflects a model prediction, not confirmed biology or clinical findings.
