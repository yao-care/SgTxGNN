# Trimetazidine: From an Unspecified Original Indication to Endolymphatic Hydrops

## One-Sentence Summary

Trimetazidine is a metabolic modulator marketed in Singapore under several brand names, but the approved indication text is not recorded in the data compiled for this report.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Endolymphatic Hydrops** (the condition underlying Ménière's disease), and **5 publications** (including two 1990 double-blind comparisons against betahistine) are linked to this direction, with **no registered clinical trials**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacological knowledge, trimetazidine is a partial inhibitor of long-chain 3-ketoacyl-CoA thiolase. It shifts cellular energy metabolism away from fatty acid oxidation toward glucose oxidation, which helps cells maintain energy under ischaemic stress.

It is also thought to improve cochlear and vestibular microcirculation and to protect inner-ear cells from ischaemic and oxidative stress. Endolymphatic hydrops underlies Ménière's disease, which causes recurrent vertigo, tinnitus and hearing loss. This offers a plausible link between trimetazidine's cellular-protective effects and inner-ear disorders.

The supporting studies are old (1990–1999) and their methodological quality, sample sizes and results cannot be verified from the available abstracts. The link should be considered plausible but unconfirmed.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Endolymphatic hydrops has the best support among the predictions, with two older double-blind comparisons against betahistine. However, their size, endpoints and results cannot be verified, no trials are registered, and the approved indications and safety information in Singapore are missing. The other predictions rest on model scores alone. The hydrops finding is best treated as a research question rather than a basis for clinical use.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Full-text review of PMIDs 2240995, 2240994 and 10367069 to confirm design, sample size, endpoints and results
- The HSA package insert, to establish the approved indications, warnings and contraindications (this is a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- A search for newer controlled trials of trimetazidine in Ménière's disease or vestibular vertigo
- A comparison with current standard treatment (e.g., betahistine)
