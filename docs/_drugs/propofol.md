## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Based on general pharmacology, propofol is a GABA-A positive allosteric modulator. Its efficacy as an anesthetic and sedative is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to acute migraine.

A preclinical study (PMID 22390898) found that propofol hemisuccinate suppresses cortical spreading depression (CSD). CSD is believed to be the neural correlate of migraine aura and a possible trigger for migraine pain. This offers a plausible basis for an abortive effect. Early clinical reports, including a 2000 outpatient headache-center series, described benefit in intractable migraine.

The clinical evidence is limited to acute rescue in monitored emergency settings, mostly in refractory cases. An editorial (PMID 32705803) questions routine use. An adult ED randomized trial (PMID 33070469, listed under the related brainstem-aura prediction) compared propofol with placebo.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: No interaction records were found in the queried data.
- **Setting**: Any migraine use should be restricted to settings with sedation monitoring and airway support.
- **Caution flag**: Case reports describe coronary artery spasm first noted or induced during propofol anesthesia, so caution applies in patients with vasospastic angina.

Please refer to the package insert for key warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
One completed Phase 2/3 trial and several randomized trials, pediatric and adult, directly test propofol for acute migraine, and a plausible mechanism exists via CSD suppression. No Phase 3 trial is confirmed, results were not supplied, and the editorial questions routine use, so use should be limited to monitored emergency settings.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert warnings and contraindications (blocking for safety screening)

- Confirmed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Singapore approved indication text, to confirm the original indication
- A monitoring protocol covering sedation depth, airway support and cardiovascular risk
