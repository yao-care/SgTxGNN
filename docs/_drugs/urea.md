# Urea: From Topical Skin Care to Dermatitis

## One-Sentence Summary

Urea is a topical moisturising and keratolytic agent. All four Singapore products are creams, but their registration records list no approved indication.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **dermatitis** (score 98.2%).
Of **50 retrieved clinical trials** and **20 publications**, only a handful test urea directly. The strongest evidence is for dry skin, hand dermatitis and radiation dermatitis prevention.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known pharmacology, urea is a humectant and keratolytic and a component of the skin's natural moisturising factor. Applied topically, it plausibly improves stratum corneum hydration and barrier function.

Dermatitis often involves a damaged skin barrier and dryness, which is where these properties would help. The literature supports use in dry skin, hand dermatitis, atopic dermatitis and radiation dermatitis prevention. A urea-containing solution was also tested for seborrhoeic dermatitis of the scalp.

There are two caveats:
- In the key Phase 3 trial NCT02251392, urea is the comparator rather than the investigational agent, which weakens attribution of efficacy.
- Several retrieved papers concern hydroxyurea or imidazolidinyl urea. These are different compounds and are not evidence for urea.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Urea has a plausible mechanism and several randomised or controlled studies in atopic, hand and radiation dermatitis. However, urea is often a comparator or vehicle component rather than the tested agent, and safety data are missing.

Other predicted indications (ranks 2–10) have little or no supporting evidence and should stay on Hold. Acne is a research question only, since urea there was a vehicle excipient.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Labelled indications for the four Singapore products
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation that NCT06483477 includes a urea arm
- Restriction to topical formulations, with the dermatitis subtype specified (atopic, hand, radiation or seborrhoeic)
