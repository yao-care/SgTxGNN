## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for imidapril. Based on known information, imidapril is an angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitor. Its efficacy in hypertension is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to pulmonary hypertension.

ACE inhibition lowers angiotensin II, which could plausibly affect the tone and remodeling of pulmonary blood vessels. However, no imidapril-specific data were provided for this condition. The high score probably reflects proximity to hypertension-related nodes in the knowledge graph rather than direct biological evidence. This link is a hypothesis to test, not a supported finding.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has a very high model score but no trials, no literature and no mechanism data, so it stays at L5. The current retrieval offers nothing to move it forward.

Other predicted indications show the same pattern. Two are class-overlap indications: malignant renovascular hypertension and malignant hypertensive renal disease. In renovascular disease, ACE inhibitors carry a known renal risk in bilateral renal artery stenosis. For the "pulmonary hypertension owing to lung disease and/or hypoxia" prediction, the 20 retrieved papers concern general hypoxia biology and do not mention imidapril, so the literature count is not real evidence. The only indication with imidapril-specific data is cerebrovascular disorder (L4, animal studies only), which could be a separate research question.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the HSA (currently blocking safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data for imidapril from DrugBank
- A targeted search for imidapril or ACE inhibitor studies in pulmonary hypertension
