# Nivolumab: From Advanced Melanoma to Non-Cutaneous Melanoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Nivolumab is a PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor that is already established for advanced melanoma, mainly the cutaneous type.
The TxGNN model predicts it may also be effective for **non-cutaneous melanoma** (mucosal, uveal and acral subtypes), with **50 clinical trials** and **8 publications** linked to this direction.
Most of this evidence covers melanoma in general rather than the non-cutaneous subset specifically.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available from DrugBank. Based on known information, nivolumab blocks PD-1 and restores T-cell cytotoxicity against PD-L1-expressing melanoma cells.

Its efficacy in advanced melanoma is established, so this prediction is largely a subtype extension. The same immune-checkpoint mechanism should apply to melanomas arising outside the skin.

Response rates in non-cutaneous disease are typically lower than in cutaneous melanoma. This is one reason the trials in this area mostly test combinations, such as nivolumab with ipilimumab, axitinib or cabozantinib.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information. No drug interactions were found in the queried data.

Published cohort and case-report data show that immune-related adverse events are an important risk with PD-1 blockade, including rheumatic and gastrointestinal events.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Nivolumab is established in advanced melanoma, and completed Phase 1/2 and Phase 3 studies plus observational cohorts support the general direction. Direct evidence specific to non-cutaneous melanoma is still limited. Response rates in this subset are generally lower than in cutaneous melanoma, and the best-matched trials (for example NCT05384496) are small.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert warnings and contraindications (currently a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Approved-indication text for the three Singapore registrations
- Mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Subtype-specific efficacy data for mucosal, uveal and acral melanoma, such as pooled analyses or results from the mucosal melanoma trials
- A safety monitoring plan for immune-related adverse events, particularly if nivolumab is combined with ipilimumab or other agents
