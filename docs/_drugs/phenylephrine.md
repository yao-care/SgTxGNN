## Phenylephrine: Nasal cavity disease
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Phenylephrine is an alpha-1 adrenergic agonist originally used as a decongestant for allergic rhinitis, the common cold, pharyngitis and conjunctivitis, and in other conditions such as haemorrhoids.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **nasal cavity disease**.
The evidence is limited: **no registered clinical trials** were found for this indication, and the **5 publications** retrieved are mostly reviews, clinical reports and combination-product studies.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, phenylephrine is a selective alpha-1 adrenergic agonist. It constricts blood vessels in the mucosa, which reduces swelling and congestion.

The predicted indication is largely an extension of its existing nasal uses (rhinitis, nasopharyngitis, common cold). Nasal mucosal decongestion is also used topically in nasal surgery to reduce bleeding, so biological plausibility is high. However, "nasal cavity disease" is a broad category, and the prediction does not specify which condition would benefit.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug interactions** (100 found; moderate-level examples): antidiabetic drugs (acarbose, glimepiride, glipizide, glyburide, canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, exenatide, human insulin), anticholinergics (atropine ophthalmic, hyoscyamine, clidinium) and diethylpropion.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The mechanism is plausible and the drug is widely marketed in Singapore, but there are no registered trials for nasal cavity disease. The retrieved literature is largely about combination products, reviews or unrelated topics, so evidence is at L4. The trigeminal autonomic cephalalgia literature is diagnostic pupillometry, not therapeutic.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the HSA
- Detailed mechanism of action data (MOA)
- Definition of a specific nasal condition (for example acute rhinosinusitis) and controlled trials of phenylephrine alone against placebo or standard care
- Safety review for patients with hypertension, cardiovascular disease and diabetes, and for paediatric use
