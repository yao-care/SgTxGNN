- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were found. Major interactions include alvimopan, bupropion, aprepitant, morphine, dexamethasone, cimetidine, clarithromycin and clotrimazole. Moderate interactions include anticholinergics (hyoscyamine, atropine, glycopyrronium, dicyclomine, clidinium), loperamide, eluxadoline, dolasetron and dronabinol. Concomitant use with other opioids, CNS depressants and CYP3A4/CYP2D6 modulators needs particular care.
- **Use-specific risks** (from the evidence review): misuse, dependence, respiratory depression and sleep-disordered breathing. The last is especially relevant in RLS, where patients have disrupted sleep.

Please refer to the package insert for full warnings and contraindications.
