## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Oxycodone is known as a mu-opioid receptor agonist used for pain relief.

Methemoglobinemia is a redox or hemoglobin-oxidation disorder, and no plausible mechanism links oxycodone to methemoglobin reduction. The high score (0.93) appears to reflect proximity in the knowledge graph rather than a real pharmacological relationship. The related prediction "methemoglobinemia, alpha type" (a hereditary hemoglobin variant) looks like an artefact of the same graph similarity.
