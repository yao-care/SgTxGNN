## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the dataset. Heparin is known to potentiate antithrombin, which inhibits clotting factors. It is an established anticoagulant, and mechanistically it may be applicable to a hypercoagulable state.

Protein C deficiency impairs a natural anticoagulant pathway and predisposes patients to thrombosis. An anticoagulant that acts through a separate pathway is therefore plausible in this setting. However, this link is a model inference only. No trials or publications were retrieved for this disease, and the registered Singapore labels in the dataset carry no indication text to compare against.
