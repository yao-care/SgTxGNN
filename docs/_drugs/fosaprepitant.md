## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the source record. Based on the evidence pack, fosaprepitant is a prodrug of aprepitant, which blocks the substance P/NK1 receptor. Its efficacy in nausea and vomiting is established.

NSIAD is a condition of excess water retention caused by gain-of-function activity of the vasopressin V2 receptor. The provided data supports no plausible link between NK1 receptor blockade and this pathway. The high TxGNN score (99.92%) is a model output only, and no trial, publication or mechanistic study was retrieved to back it. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis and not as a repurposing signal.
