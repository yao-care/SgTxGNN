## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Based on known information, canagliflozin is an SGLT2 inhibitor that lowers blood glucose by reducing renal glucose reabsorption, and its efficacy in type 2 diabetes has been established.

For the predicted indication, **no established mechanistic link** was identified. Focal stiff limb syndrome belongs to the stiff-person spectrum, an autoimmune/neurological disorder involving impaired GABAergic inhibition. SGLT2 inhibition has no known direct effect on this pathway. The high score is a graph-based prediction only. One possible explanation is that anti-GAD65 autoimmunity in the stiff-person spectrum is associated with autoimmune diabetes, which may create a link in the knowledge graph. This is an association, not evidence of efficacy.

The same score was given to classic stiff person syndrome (97.92%). Other predictions in the top 10 include opsismodysplasia, thiamine-responsive dysfunction syndrome, several localized lipodystrophies, pancreatic agenesis and autoimmune oophoritis. None of them has supporting trials or literature. Several of them look like diabetes-phenotype or lipodystrophy-cluster associations in the graph rather than therapeutic rationales.
