## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, sitagliptin belongs to the DPP-4 inhibitor class. Its efficacy in type 2 diabetes is established, and it works by raising active incretin levels to enhance glucose-dependent insulin secretion.

Opsismodysplasia is a rare skeletal dysplasia related to INPPL1, and no established link to DPP-4 inhibition has been identified. The high TxGNN score (0.989) appears to come from relationships within the knowledge graph rather than from independent clinical or biological evidence. Any mechanistic rationale is therefore speculative.

Other top-ranked predictions show the same pattern. Several have identical or near-identical scores (for example, classic stiff person syndrome and focal stiff limb syndrome), and a cluster of localized lipodystrophy conditions scores similarly. This suggests the predictions are driven by shared graph neighbours.
