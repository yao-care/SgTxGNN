## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified. Most listed ones are Moderate, including phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine, epinephrine, salbutamol, formoterol, acetazolamide, hydrochlorothiazide, estradiol, hydrocortisone, benazepril, aripiprazole, and ethanol. Hydrocortisone (topical) and alclometasone (topical) are Minor.
- **Pancreatitis signal:** A published case report describes sitagliptin-induced pancreatitis after 8 years of use. This is a safety concern, not supportive evidence.

Please refer to the package insert for full warnings and contraindications.
