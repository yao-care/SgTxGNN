## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the record. Carbidopa is a peripheral DOPA decarboxylase inhibitor. It stops levodopa from being converted to dopamine outside the brain, so more levodopa reaches the central nervous system.

Rasmussen encephalitis is an immune-mediated epileptic encephalopathy, and no clear dopaminergic link to it has been identified. The high TxGNN score (0.984) therefore reflects a pattern in the knowledge graph, not a known biological mechanism. No trials or literature were found to support the pairing.
