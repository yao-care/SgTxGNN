# Vonoprazan: From Gastric/Duodenal Ulcer and Reflux Esophagitis to Active Peptic Ulcer Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Vonoprazan is a potassium-competitive acid blocker (P-CAB), originally used for gastric ulcer, duodenal ulcer, esophagitis and gastroesophageal reflux disease.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **active peptic ulcer disease**, which is essentially an on-label or near-label use.
Currently there are **0 registered clinical trials** but **18 publications** supporting this direction, including systematic reviews, meta-analyses and randomised trials.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. From general pharmacological knowledge, vonoprazan reversibly inhibits gastric H+/K+-ATPase (the proton pump). Unlike proton pump inhibitors, it does not need acid activation. This gives rapid onset, durable 24-hour acid control, and less dependence on meal timing or CYP2C19 variability.

Active peptic ulcer disease covers the same gastric and duodenal ulcers that vonoprazan already treats. This is therefore closer to an on-label use than true repurposing. Acid suppression also supports its role in *H. pylori* eradication regimens, and the drug is used this way in the US as part of dual and triple packs.

Evidence in ulcer settings is mixed in places. A 2024 meta-analysis notes that efficacy for gastric and duodenal ulcers versus PPIs "remains controversial", although phase 3 trials showed non-inferiority to lansoprazole.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction query completed, but the only record is pharmacological target information (vonoprazan acting on ATP4A, the gastric proton pump), not a drug-drug interaction.
- **Long-term use**: Published case reports describe gastric mucosal changes, mucosal redness, white globe appearance lesions, and severe rebound acid hypersecretion after long-term use (e.g., PMID 39712905). These are single cases but are relevant to monitoring.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.
