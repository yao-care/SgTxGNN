# Mupirocin: From Topical Bacterial Skin Infection to Pleural Empyema

## One-Sentence Summary

Mupirocin is a topical antibiotic ointment, used mainly against staphylococcal skin infections.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Mupirocin inhibits bacterial isoleucyl-tRNA synthetase, which blocks protein synthesis in susceptible bacteria, mainly gram-positive cocci such as *Staphylococcus aureus*. It is used as a topical agent with negligible systemic exposure.

Pleural empyema is a bacterial infection of the pleural space. In principle, an antibacterial drug could be relevant, which may explain the high model score. In practice the fit is poor. Empyema is treated with drainage plus systemic antibiotics that reach the pleural space. A topical ointment cannot achieve that exposure.

The score should therefore be read as a knowledge-graph association, not as evidence of clinical benefit. No supporting studies were found.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on the model score alone, with no trials or publications. Mupirocin is a topical agent and cannot deliver the systemic or pleural-space exposure that empyema treatment requires.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The HSA package insert (warnings and contraindications), which is currently missing and blocks safety screening
- Confirmation of the approved indications for the Singapore licenses
- A route-compatibility assessment, since no systemic formulation is available
- A pharmacological rationale for pleural-space exposure, plus any supporting studies

**Note on other candidates in this pack:** Rank 1 has the weakest evidence. **Staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome** (rank 9, L3) has much stronger support:
- Its mechanism fits: mupirocin is established for topical staphylococcal skin infection.
- It has a 2023 retrospective cohort of IV antibiotics combined with 2% mupirocin ointment, plus case series and outbreak reports.
- There is no RCT. Mupirocin-resistant clones have also been reported.

Consider evaluating it as a separate candidate.
