# Bleomycin: From Antineoplastic Use to Cauda Equina Neoplasm

## One-Sentence Summary

Bleomycin is a cytotoxic anticancer antibiotic. Its original indication is not stated in the Singapore registration record.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **cauda equina neoplasm**, but only **0 clinical trials** and **3 indirect publications** (one case report and two cohort or case-series studies) support this. None of them tests bleomycin for this tumour.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available from DrugBank. From the evidence pack's own analysis, bleomycin causes iron-dependent DNA strand breaks. That mechanism is plausible for lymphomas or germ cell tumours that involve the central nervous system (CNS) or spine.

The score of 0.993 reflects proximity in the knowledge graph only. No cauda equina-specific bleomycin data were found. The retrieved papers describe tumours that can reach the cauda equina, such as lymphoma and CNS germinoma. They do not show that bleomycin works for a primary cauda equina neoplasm.

There is also a pharmacological obstacle. Bleomycin penetrates the blood-brain barrier poorly, which weakens the case for CNS or spinal use.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on model score alone (L5). There are no trials and no bleomycin-specific literature for cauda equina neoplasm. Poor CNS penetration and the known pulmonary toxicity argue against progressing without new evidence.

Other predicted indications in the same pack have stronger support, such as reticulum cell sarcoma (non-Hodgkin lymphoma). That use is largely on-label lymphoma therapy rather than true repurposing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The HSA package insert (warnings, contraindications and approved indications)
- Detailed mechanism of action data (DrugBank)
- Data on bleomycin penetration into the spine or CSF
- Disease-specific studies of bleomycin in primary cauda equina tumours
