# Cysteine: From Respiratory and Mucolytic Uses to Dry Eye Syndrome

## One-Sentence Summary

Cysteine is an amino acid and antioxidant precursor, originally linked to bronchitis, paracetamol poisoning, cystic fibrosis, bronchiectasis and amyloidosis.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **dry eye syndrome**, but the data compiled for this report contain **no clinical trials** and **only preclinical or formulation-type publications**, none of which test cysteine itself as a treatment.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not currently available. Cysteine is a thiol-containing amino acid and a precursor of glutathione, a major cellular antioxidant. Its established uses (respiratory conditions, paracetamol poisoning) are consistent with antioxidant and mucus-modifying properties.

Excess reactive oxygen species (ROS) are thought to drive the inflammatory cycle in dry eye disease. Thiol groups also bind to mucus glycoproteins, which is why cysteine-modified materials show strong mucoadhesion. This gives a plausible rationale based on oxidative stress and ocular surface adhesion.

However, the retrieved publications involve cysteine-derived materials (e.g. cysteine-modified chitosan, hyaluronic acid-cysteine ethyl ester, a persulfide prodrug), not cysteine given as a drug. The link is therefore indirect.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is very high, but support is limited to preclinical and formulation studies involving cysteine-derived materials, with no clinical trials. The registered Singapore products are intravenous nutrition solutions, not ocular formulations, so the prediction remains a research question.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data for cysteine (DrugBank)
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the HSA website
- Direct preclinical evidence testing cysteine itself (not derivatives or conjugates) in dry eye models
- Assessment of a suitable ophthalmic formulation and route, plus ocular safety and tolerability data
- Evidence-based comparison against established dry eye therapies before any clinical study is designed
