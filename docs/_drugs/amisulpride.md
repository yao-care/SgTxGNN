## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction screen returned 100 interacting drugs, of which 20 are listed in the compiled data. Several are rated **Major**: cisapride, clarithromycin, dolasetron, granisetron, levofloxacin and metoclopramide. Many of these are QT-prolonging or related agents, which is consistent with amisulpride's known QTc risk. Moderate interactions include famotidine, epinephrine, hydrocortisone, amphotericin B, and several laxatives and antidiarrhoeals (bisacodyl, castor oil, glycerin, lactitol, lactulose, loperamide, magnesium citrate and hydroxide). Metronidazole is rated Minor.
- **Other safety signals in the literature**: hyperprolactinaemia and QTc prolongation are the main concerns. Renal impairment requires dose adjustment. Amisulpride has a relatively favourable metabolic and weight profile compared with olanzapine and clozapine.

The package insert warnings and contraindications were not available for this report. Please refer to the package insert for the full safety information.
