# Fluorometholone: From Ocular Inflammatory Conditions to Postinfectious Vasculitis

## One-Sentence Summary

Fluorometholone is a topical ophthalmic corticosteroid with anti-inflammatory activity, marketed in Singapore as an eye suspension.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **postinfectious vasculitis**, but **no clinical trials and no publications** currently support this prediction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Fluorometholone is a topical corticosteroid used in the eye, and corticosteroids broadly suppress inflammation. A postinfectious vasculitis is an immune-mediated inflammatory condition, which is the only mechanistic link to the prediction.

That link is weak. Postinfectious vasculitis is usually systemic. Ophthalmic dosing gives very low systemic exposure, so a meaningful benefit is unlikely. The prediction is best read as a statistical signal from the knowledge graph, not a credible treatment hypothesis.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**

- For the ocular candidates, a safety review of steroid use in active infection, including delayed healing and raised intraocular pressure.
