## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not currently available. Choline salicylate is a non-acetylated salicylate, a class known for analgesic and anti-inflammatory activity through effects on prostanoid pathways.

A plausible but unproven link is that prostanoid modulation could influence the vasospasm underlying Prinzmetal angina. However, this link is uncertain. High-dose aspirin has been reported to potentially worsen vasospastic angina, and non-acetylated salicylates have minimal antiplatelet activity, which weakens the rationale. The high score reflects a model inference, not demonstrated clinical benefit.
