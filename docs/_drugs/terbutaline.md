## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Terbutaline is a selective beta2-adrenergic agonist. It relaxes airway smooth muscle through the cAMP pathway, which is the established basis of bronchodilation in obstructive airway disease. Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank are not available. This explanation therefore rests on the drug's known pharmacology.

The predicted indication closely matches terbutaline's known use against bronchospasm, so the prediction is biologically coherent. It may be an on-label use rather than a genuinely new one. Terbutaline appears in the evidence mostly as a comparator, for example in asthma studies of budesonide/formoterol. Direct trials with terbutaline as the study intervention are limited, and the supplied metadata cannot confirm them.
