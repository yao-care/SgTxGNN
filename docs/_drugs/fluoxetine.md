## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, fluoxetine is an SSRI. Serotonergic modulation may reduce affective instability, obsessional features and impulsivity, which overlap with some schizotypal traits.

The supporting literature is limited. A 12-week, non-blind prospective study of 22 patients with borderline or schizotypal personality disorder (or both) reported significant reductions in self-injury and in Hopkins Symptom Checklist scores (PMID 1853957). Mixed samples and the lack of a control group limit what can be concluded for schizotypal personality disorder specifically. A cluster A pharmacotherapy review (PMID 29955451) and a psychopharmacology review (PMID 9448667) note that there is no agent of choice for this disorder. No effect on core psychotic-like or negative symptoms has been established.

## Safety Considerations

- **Safety signal in the target population:** one case report describes transient psychosis with psychogenic polydipsia in a schizotypal patient taking fluoxetine (PMID 9664779).
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were recorded. Major-level interactions include bupropion, lorcaserin, diethylpropion, dolasetron, eliglustat, palonosetron and cisapride. Moderate-level interactions include famotidine, loperamide, morphine, acetylsalicylic acid, clarithromycin and several laxatives.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The TxGNN score is very high, but the evidence is at level L3. It rests on a single small, non-blind study in a mixed borderline/schizotypal sample, plus reviews that identify no preferred agent. There are no registered clinical trials, and a case report raises a safety signal.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A controlled trial of fluoxetine specifically in schizotypal personality disorder
- Mechanism-of-action data and package-insert warnings and contraindications
- Assessment of the psychosis-related safety signal and of the interaction profile in this population
