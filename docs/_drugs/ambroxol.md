## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, ambroxol increases airway mucus clearance and has reported local anaesthetic (sodium channel blocking) and anti-inflammatory properties. These could plausibly benefit upper airway conditions, including those of the nasal cavity.

The only linked publication is a review on treating acute cough in respiratory viral infections. It does not directly study nasal cavity disease, so the mechanistic link rests on general pharmacology rather than on direct data. The prediction should be treated as a research question, not a demonstrated benefit.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: One pharmacological interaction entry was found, with the target glucosylceramidase beta (GBA1). Ambroxol acts as a chaperone of this enzyme. It inhibits enzyme activity at neutral pH in acellular fluid, but increases intracellular activity by raising the amount of enzyme reaching lysosomes. No clinical interaction severity level was provided.

Please refer to the package insert for other safety information, including warnings and contraindications.
