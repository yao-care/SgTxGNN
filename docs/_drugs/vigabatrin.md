## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data for vigabatrin is not available in this Evidence Pack (Data Gap DG002). Based on the supporting literature, vigabatrin is an antiepileptic drug used clinically for refractory seizures and infantile spasms — this is corroborated by case reports within the evidence set describing its use for West syndrome and hypsarrhythmia (e.g., PMID 36436329, PMID 41004695).

ARX-related X-linked intellectual disability with seizures is a genetic neurodevelopmental disorder in which epilepsy (including infantile spasms) is a core presenting feature. Mechanistic studies included in this evidence pack show that the ARX gene is a transcription factor essential for the development of GABAergic neurons in the forebrain (PMID 19605412), and that ARX mutations produce lissencephaly-like and epilepsy-like phenotypes in animal models. Since vigabatrin acts on the GABAergic system, there is a plausible theoretical mechanistic link between vigabatrin and seizure control in ARX-related disease — however, no study in this evidence pack directly tests vigabatrin in ARX-related XLID patients, so this remains a hypothesis-generating signal rather than a validated indication.
