## Safety Considerations

- **Disease associations flagged in the knowledge graph (not label text):** kidney disease, pyoureter, congestive heart failure, peripheral nervous system disease, peripheral neuropathy, anaemia, macrocytic anaemia and scotoma (visual field defect). The scotoma flag is consistent with the known retinal and visual field toxicity of vigabatrin, which is a particular concern in young infants.
- **Drug interactions:** 65 interactions were identified. Major interactions include systemic and other corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, prednisone, prednisolone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone, budesonide), oxyquinoline (topical) and deferoxamine. Moderate interactions include morphine, opium, diphenoxylate, difenoxin, dronabinol, nabilone, metoclopramide, sibutramine and ethanol.
- **Case report signal:** One case report describes vigabatrin-associated MRI abnormalities with obstructive hydrocephalus in an infant with lissencephaly.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.
