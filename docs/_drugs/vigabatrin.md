# Vigabatrin: From Infantile Spasms to ARX-Related X-Linked Intellectual Disability

## One-Sentence Summary

Vigabatrin is an established treatment for infantile spasms. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **X-linked intellectual disability, with or without seizures, ARX-related**. ## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Vigabatrin irreversibly inhibits GABA-transaminase, which raises brain GABA levels. ARX is a transcription factor that drives the development of GABAergic interneurons, so ARX-related disease is considered an interneuronopathy. Infantile spasms are a common seizure phenotype of ARX mutations, as shown in several case reports.

The plausible link is therefore **symptomatic**. Vigabatrin could help control the seizure component, especially infantile spasms. This does not show that it treats the underlying ARX disorder or the intellectual disability. The retrieved literature does not report ARX-specific vigabatrin outcomes.

The nearest supporting evidence comes from related conditions. These include case-level vigabatrin use in LIS1-related lissencephaly and in West syndrome due to pyruvate dehydrogenase deficiency. The 2008 case series of ARX mutations describes a patient with refractory infantile spasms but does not report a vigabatrin response.

## Safety Considerations

- **Key Warnings**: Retinal toxicity (visual field loss) is a key concern, particularly in infants and with long-term use. Vigabatrin-associated MRI changes have also been reported in an infant (PMID 41004695), generally reversible on cessation of the medication.

No drug interaction records were found. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is high, but the evidence is limited to mechanistic plausibility, reviews, case reports and preclinical work, with no trials. The rationale is symptomatic seizure control only, and the retinal toxicity risk in infants is significant. The other nine predicted indications (for example episodic kinesigenic dyskinesia and several CDG subtypes) are model predictions only (L5) and are also on Hold.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Singapore package insert warnings and contraindications (HSA), which are required for safety screening
- Mechanism of action data confirmed from DrugBank
- Published outcomes of vigabatrin in ARX-related patients, such as case series or registry data
- A risk-benefit assessment of retinal toxicity in the target infant population
- Confirmation of the approved indication text for SIN07955P
