## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, loratadine is a long-acting antihistamine that selectively blocks peripheral histamine H1 receptors. Its efficacy in allergic rhinitis and idiopathic urticaria is established.

In allergic urticaria, histamine released from mast cells drives the characteristic wheal and flare, so H1 blockade directly addresses the pathway. Idiopathic urticaria is already a labelled indication, and the allergic form shares the same histamine-mediated mechanism.

Reviews in the compiled literature report that loratadine is effective in chronic urticaria and as effective as several other antihistamines in controlled comparative studies.
