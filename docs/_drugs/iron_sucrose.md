## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Iron sucrose is an intravenous iron replacement product, and the supplied literature shows it corrects iron deficiency in CKD and dialysis patients. No mechanistic link to primary hyperoxaluria, an inherited disorder of oxalate metabolism, can be established from the supplied data.

The high score (98.82%) reflects an association in the TxGNN knowledge graph and does not amount to biological or clinical evidence. There are no trials, publications or preclinical studies linking iron sucrose to primary hyperoxaluria. Until a mechanistic hypothesis is supported by data, this prediction should be treated as a research lead only.
