## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, palbociclib is a CDK4/6 inhibitor whose efficacy in HR+/HER2- breast cancer has been established. However, no established link exists between CDK4/6 inhibition and excess thyroid hormone.

The prediction rests only on the model's knowledge-graph similarity. No trials or publications support it, and no plausible mechanism has been identified. The score is very high (99.44%), but it ranks only 6,962 overall. It should be treated as a hypothesis, not evidence of benefit.

Other predicted indications for palbociclib have stronger support than hyperthyroidism:
- **Rheumatoid arthritis (L4):** CDK6-dependent synovial fibroblast hyperplasia in mouse arthritis models, plus one case report of improvement on palbociclib.
- **Myeloid leukemia (L2):** several Phase 1 and Phase 1/2 trials, including a completed CPX-351 plus palbociclib study in AML (NCT03844997).
