## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on the model score alone, with no clinical trials, no literature and no plausible mechanism for migraine. Evidence is at L5, and the pack stops at S0 (initial screening).

Among the other predicted indications, only hyperthyroidism has trial evidence (two Phase 2 trials, L3, "Research Question"). Those trials test rhTSH as a pretreatment to radioiodine in goiter, not as a hyperthyroidism treatment, and TSH stimulation may worsen thyrotoxicosis. It is therefore a safety question rather than support for repurposing.
