# Ticagrelor: From Acute Coronary Syndrome to Intracranial Arteriosclerosis

## One-Sentence Summary

Ticagrelor is an oral antiplatelet drug that is generally used to prevent clots in acute coronary syndrome and after heart attack. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **intracranial arteriosclerosis** (narrowing of arteries inside the brain). Support so far is **10 clinical trials** (most are indirect, and none has reported ticagrelor results in this disease) and **3 publications**.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the record. Based on known information, ticagrelor is a P2Y12 platelet inhibitor. It reduces platelet-driven clotting on damaged artery walls, and its efficacy in coronary artery disease is established. Mechanistically, it may be applicable to atherosclerotic narrowing of brain arteries.

Intracranial atherosclerosis and coronary disease share the same underlying process: plaque in the artery wall triggers platelet clumping, which can block the vessel. Ticagrelor is also already used in stroke and TIA settings and in neurointerventional dual antiplatelet regimens. The model's prediction is therefore biologically plausible.

However, no completed Phase 3 trial specifically tests ticagrelor in intracranial atherosclerosis. The strongest direct evidence is an ongoing Phase 3 trial (CAPTIVA) with no results yet.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information. No drug interaction records were found in the data. As with other antiplatelet drugs, bleeding risk is the main concern. This matters especially in the brain, where intracranial bleeding is one of the CAPTIVA trial's endpoints.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high and the mechanism is plausible. However, the evidence is still at the L3 level (a small observational cohort and a trial design paper). The only direct trial, CAPTIVA, has no results yet, and no completed Phase 3 trial tests ticagrelor in intracranial atherosclerosis.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- CAPTIVA results, particularly the ticagrelor arm's stroke and intracranial bleeding outcomes
- The Singapore package insert (warnings, contraindications, approved indications), which is a blocking gap for safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation of the original approved indication for the Singapore registrations
