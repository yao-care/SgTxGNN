## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Based on known information, rasagiline is a selective MAO-B inhibitor. PLA2G6-associated neurodegeneration includes a dystonia-parkinsonism form (PARK14) with dopaminergic involvement. Blocking dopamine breakdown through MAO-B inhibition could plausibly ease parkinsonian symptoms in these patients.
