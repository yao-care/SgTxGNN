# Midostaurin: From Acute Myeloid Leukaemia to Familial Thrombocytosis

## One-Sentence Summary

Midostaurin is an oral multikinase inhibitor originally used for acute myeloid leukaemia (AML).
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Familial Thrombocytosis**,
but **no clinical trials** and **no publications** currently support this specific prediction.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, midostaurin is a multikinase inhibitor that acts on targets including FLT3, KIT and PKC. Its efficacy in AML is tied to this kinase inhibition, and mechanistically it may be applicable to certain kinase-driven blood disorders.

Familial thrombocytosis is usually caused by inherited variants in genes such as THPO, MPL or JAK2. A link to midostaurin's kinase targets is plausible, but no trial or publication was retrieved to support it. The high TxGNN score may partly reflect proximity in the knowledge network rather than a proven biological connection.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on the model score alone (L5). No trials or publications address familial thrombocytosis. The one clinical study found for another predicted indication, metastatic melanoma, was negative. Familial thrombocytosis is rare, and its link to midostaurin's kinase targets is only theoretical.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data for midostaurin, to analyse the link to thrombocytosis-related pathways
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator (HSA), which are required before safety screening
- Preclinical or case-level evidence, such as testing in THPO-, MPL- or JAK2-variant models
- Review of whether thrombocythaemia (L4, myeloproliferative neoplasm context) is a stronger candidate than familial thrombocytosis
- Biomarker-selected evidence (for example, KIT-mutant disease) before reconsidering any melanoma indication
