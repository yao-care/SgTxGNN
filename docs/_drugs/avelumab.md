## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Avelumab is generally known as an antibody that blocks PD-L1, which can release the brake on T-cell attack against tumour cells.

Tumours linked to human herpesvirus 8 (HHV-8), such as Kaposi sarcoma, are driven by a virus and may express PD-L1. Blocking PD-L1 is therefore biologically plausible. This is a hypothesis only: no trial or publication was supplied to confirm it, and the TxGNN score alone cannot establish clinical benefit.
