## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Diphenoxylate is a mu-opioid agonist. It reduces peristalsis and slows gastrointestinal motility, which is the basis for its use in non-infectious diarrhea. Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available from DrugBank in this evidence pack, so this description rests on the pharmacological assessment in the pack.

Diarrhea and gastroduodenitis are both gastrointestinal conditions, but they are treated differently. Diphenoxylate relieves a symptom (rapid transit) and does not treat mucosal inflammation of the stomach and duodenum. No mechanistic link to gastroduodenitis was identified.

The high score (0.908) reflects a pattern in the knowledge graph, not evidence that the drug works here. No trials or publications support this specific prediction. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis with low plausibility until independent evidence appears.
