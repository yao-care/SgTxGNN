## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Naproxen is a COX-1/COX-2 inhibitor in the NSAID class. It is known to reduce prostaglandin-mediated inflammation and pain.

Brachydactyly-syndactyly syndrome is a congenital limb malformation, so it is a developmental disorder rather than an inflammatory one. No plausible mechanism links COX inhibition to correcting or modifying this condition. The high score (99.35%, rank 7,813 among all predictions) is a statistical association in the knowledge graph. Nothing in the available data supports a biological rationale.
