## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Alfentanil is known as a mu-opioid receptor agonist.

The mechanistic link to the predicted disease is weak. Opioids can influence vasopressin release, but nephrogenic syndrome of inappropriate antidiuresis is caused by gain-of-function mutations in the vasopressin V2 receptor (AVPR2). This is a renal receptor defect that works independently of vasopressin secretion. A drug acting on vasopressin release would therefore not be expected to correct it. The high model score is not backed by any trial or literature evidence.
