## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Ambrisentan is known as an endothelin type A receptor antagonist, which reduces vasoconstriction and vascular proliferation in the pulmonary circulation.

Pulmonary arteriovenous malformations (commonly seen in hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia, HHT) involve abnormal vascular remodelling, which is the plausible reason the model links the two. However, the only linked publication describes pulmonary arterial hypertension coexisting with HHT, not treatment of the malformation itself.

Endothelin blockade is not known to close or shrink such malformations, and vasodilation could theoretically worsen right-to-left shunting. The high score most likely reflects the shared pulmonary vascular and HHT-PAH context rather than a demonstrated therapeutic effect.

Minor-level interactions include clarithromycin, miconazole and omeprazole. Others, such as metformin, simvastatin, prednisone and morphine, are of unknown severity.
