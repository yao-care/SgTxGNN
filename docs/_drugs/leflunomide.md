## One-Sentence Summary

Leflunomide is an oral immunomodulatory drug used for rheumatoid arthritis. ## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the Evidence Pack. From the literature, leflunomide's active metabolite A77 1726 (teriflunomide) inhibits dihydroorotate dehydrogenase (DHODH). This depletes pyrimidines and suppresses rapidly dividing immune cells, which is the basis of its use in rheumatoid arthritis.

For brachydactyly-syndactyly syndrome, no mechanistic link could be identified. It is a rare developmental disorder, and DHODH inhibition is not a plausible way to treat it. The very high score most likely reflects the structure of the knowledge graph rather than pharmacology. Treat this prediction as a hypothesis with no supporting evidence.
