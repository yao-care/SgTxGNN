# Nadroparin: From Anticoagulant Use to Primary Release Disorder of Platelets

## One-Sentence Summary

Nadroparin is a low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH), an anticoagulant that works through antithrombin to inhibit factor Xa.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **primary release disorder of platelets**,

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Based on known information, nadroparin is an LMWH. It potentiates antithrombin and inhibits factor Xa, which reduces thrombus formation and propagation.

The prediction does not hold up mechanistically. Primary release disorder of platelets is a platelet function disorder with a bleeding tendency. Anticoagulation would be expected to increase bleeding risk rather than treat the condition. The high score (98.15%) most likely reflects proximity in the knowledge graph through coagulation-related nodes, not a therapeutic rationale.
