## Safety Considerations

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available for this report. Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

The knowledge graph lists the following conditions as contraindication-related for dinoprostone. These come from graph relationships, not from label text, and should be verified against the official label:
- **Cardiovascular and blood pressure:** hypertension, hypotensive disorder, cardiovascular disease, heart disease
- **Respiratory:** asthma (intrinsic and allergic), lung disease
- **Metabolic:** diabetes mellitus, diabetic ketoacidosis
- **Organ disease:** kidney disease, liver disease, gallbladder disease
- **Neurological and eye:** epilepsy, glaucoma, ocular hypertension
- **Blood:** anaemia, disseminated intravascular coagulation, purpura fulminans
- **Gynaecological and infectious:** uterine leiomyoma, cervicitis, genital herpes, pyoureter

No drug-interaction data were found.
