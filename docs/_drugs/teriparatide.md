## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the record. Teriparatide is a PTH analog that acts on bone remodelling and calcium homeostasis. Its established role is stimulating bone formation in osteoporosis.

The link to duodenal ulcer is weak. Duodenal ulcers arise from impaired mucosal defence and acid-related injury, and the PTH receptor pathway has no known role in either. The high TxGNN score therefore looks like an unsupported graph-based association, not a mechanistically grounded signal.

The other top-ranked predictions (esophageal malformation, duodenal obstruction, duodenogastric reflux, and others) are also unsupported. Of the ten predictions, only one has meaningful support: **pregnancy-associated osteoporosis** (rank 8, score 99.55%, evidence level L3). It is covered in the conclusion below.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The top predicted indication, duodenal ulcer, rests only on a model score. There are no trials or publications, and no plausible mechanism. The nine other predictions are similar, except pregnancy-associated osteoporosis. That one has observational support (a teriparatide cohort study, PMID 34132853, plus several reviews and case reports) but no RCT, so it would at best be a "Proceed with Guardrails" candidate.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Do not pursue duodenal ulcer further unless new mechanistic or clinical evidence emerges
- Evaluate pregnancy- and lactation-associated osteoporosis as a separate, better-supported candidate, with specialist oversight, pregnancy and lactation checks, and standard teriparatide contraindications
- Obtain the HSA package insert (warnings, contraindications, approved indication text) to complete safety screening
- Obtain detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
