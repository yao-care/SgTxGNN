## Safety Considerations

- **Package insert warnings and contraindications:** Not available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the package insert for safety information.
  - Cardiovascular: hypertension, long QT syndrome (including familial), cardiovascular and heart disease, cerebrovascular disorder.
  - Liver and kidney: liver failure, liver disease, cirrhosis, impaired renal function, gallbladder disease.
  - Neurological and psychiatric: epilepsy, bipolar disorder and major affective disorder, neuroleptic malignant syndrome.
  - Eye: angle-closure glaucoma, ocular hypertension.
  - Endocrine and metabolic: hyperthyroidism (Graves disease, thyrotoxicosis), hypertriglyceridemia, inappropriate ADH syndrome.
  - Other: blood coagulation disease, substance-related disorders and drug dependence, interstitial lung disease.
- **Drug interactions:** 100 interactions were recorded. Those flagged **Major** among the entries reviewed:
  - Bupropion
  - Lorcaserin
  - Diethylpropion
  - Phentermine
  - Dolasetron
  - Palonosetron
  
  Moderate-level examples include aspirin, morphine, epinephrine, clarithromycin, famotidine, loperamide, and aprepitant.
- **Population concern:** Ohdo syndrome mainly affects children, and no paediatric safety or efficacy data exist for this use.
