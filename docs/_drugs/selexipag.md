# Selexipag: From Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension to PAH Associated with Congenital Heart Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Selexipag is an oral prostacyclin receptor agonist, used for pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in adults.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **pulmonary arterial hypertension associated with congenital heart disease (CHD-PAH)**.
Support currently consists of **2 registered clinical trials** (neither with efficacy results) and **15 retrieved publications**. The strongest are a post hoc subgroup analysis of the Phase 3 GRIPHON trial and small real-world series.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism data were not available in the Evidence Pack. Based on the pipeline's analysis, selexipag's active metabolite is a selective prostacyclin IP-receptor agonist. It causes pulmonary vasodilation and has antiproliferative effects.

CHD-PAH is a WHO Group 1 PAH subtype and shares the prostacyclin-pathway deficiency seen in other forms of PAH. The new indication is therefore a subtype of the disease the drug already treats, not a distant disease area. This is why the prediction is mechanistically sensible.

An oral agent may also be useful where parenteral prostacyclin carries significant risks in CHD-PAH patients.

The main limitation is the strength of the evidence. The pipeline's L1 grade rests on a post hoc CHD-PAH subgroup analysis of the Phase 3 GRIPHON trial. Only the title of that analysis was supplied. The subgroup is small, and no completed trial was designed for CHD-PAH.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
CHD-PAH is a recognised Group 1 PAH subtype, and selexipag acts directly on the pathway involved. The supporting evidence is a post hoc subgroup analysis of a Phase 3 RCT plus small case series and cohorts. The only CHD-specific registered study was terminated at 9 participants. This supports cautious, specialist-supervised consideration but not an efficacy claim.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The HSA package insert (warnings, contraindications and approved indication), which is currently missing and blocks safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Full-text review of the GRIPHON CHD-PAH subgroup analysis (PMID 30632656), and verification of the PMID 29521655 classification
- Clinical guardrails: confirm Eisenmenger or repaired-shunt status, monitor right ventricular function and haemodynamics, and require pulmonary hypertension centre oversight
- Separate confirmation before any paediatric use, because the Phase 2 pharmacokinetic study is single-arm
