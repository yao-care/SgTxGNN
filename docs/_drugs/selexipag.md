# Selexipag: From Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension to PAH Associated with Congenital Heart Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Selexipag is an oral selective prostacyclin (IP) receptor agonist, originally used to treat pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH).
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **pulmonary arterial hypertension associated with congenital heart disease (CHD-PAH)**, with **no registered clinical trials** but **15 publications**, including a subgroup analysis of the randomised GRIPHON trial, currently supporting this direction.
Because CHD-PAH is a subgroup of the already-approved PAH indication, this is better described as a population extension than a true repurposing.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the compiled data. Selexipag is known as an oral selective IP prostacyclin receptor agonist. Its active metabolite causes pulmonary vasodilation and has antiproliferative effects on the pulmonary vasculature.

CHD-PAH belongs to Group 1 PAH, the same group as the already-approved indication. These vasodilatory and antiproliferative effects are therefore expected to apply regardless of PAH cause. Selexipag is also oral, which may avoid the risks of parenteral prostacyclin therapy that are a concern in CHD patients.

The supporting evidence is largely a GRIPHON subgroup analysis of patients with corrected CHD-PAH, plus case series and real-world reports (including Eisenmenger syndrome and paediatric patients). No dedicated trial exists for this subgroup.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
CHD-PAH is a subgroup of the approved PAH indication, and the GRIPHON subgroup analysis plus multiple case series and real-world reports support use. However, there is no dedicated trial in this population, so the evidence rests on subgroup and observational data.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from HSA (currently a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Approved indication text for the Singapore licences, to confirm whether CHD-PAH is already covered
- Dedicated or prospective data in CHD-PAH, including Eisenmenger syndrome and corrected versus uncorrected defects
- A review of drug interactions relevant to CHD-PAH patients
