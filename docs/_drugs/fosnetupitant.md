## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Fosnetupitant is a prodrug of netupitant, an NK1 receptor antagonist. Its efficacy in CINV is established, but there is no established mechanistic link to leprosy.

The only conceivable connection is speculative. Substance P-driven neurogenic inflammation might relate to leprosy neuropathy. Netupitant has no antimicrobial activity against *M. leprae*, so it cannot treat the infection itself. The high score (0.964) is a graph-based prediction and does not reflect a demonstrated biological rationale.

The other nine top-ranked predictions are also L5 with no trials or publications. They include mycotic corneal ulcer, coronary artery disease, candidiasis, myocardial ischemia and several polyp-type conditions. Most look like knowledge-graph proximity artifacts rather than real pharmacology.
