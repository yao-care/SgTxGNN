## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available for Trospium in this evidence pack. Based on the mechanistic annotation provided alongside the prediction, Trospium is described as a quaternary ammonium antimuscarinic agent (M3 receptor antagonist), a class of drugs that theoretically inhibits smooth muscle spasm — a plausible pharmacological rationale for diarrhea-predominant IBS (IBS-D) symptoms.

However, the relationship between any established indication and IBS cannot be verified here, since no original indication text, brand information, or Singapore license data exists for this drug. The only literature cited for this prediction (PMID 33890538) examines antimuscarinic use for overactive bladder (OAB) in older adults with dementia — it does not address IBS at all. As the evidence pack itself notes, the mechanistic link to IBS is "purely speculative" (機轉關聯僅屬推測), and this candidate should be treated as a hypothesis generated by the knowledge graph rather than a clinically supported repurposing signal.
