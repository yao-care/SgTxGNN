  - **Major:** corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, triamcinolone including ophthalmic, dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, prednisone, prednisolone) and clarithromycin.

- **Malignancy signals in the literature:** A case report describes CML arising during tofacitinib therapy (PMID 31263618), and a pharmacovigilance study links immunosuppressive medications with sebaceous carcinoma (PMID 38071595). These are signals and not established causal findings, but they deserve attention in any oncology repurposing.
