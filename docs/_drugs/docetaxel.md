# Docetaxel: From Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer to Female Breast Carcinoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Docetaxel is a taxane chemotherapy drug originally used for non-small cell lung cancer, prostate cancer, head and neck cancer, gastric cancer and breast cancer.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **female breast carcinoma**, but breast carcinoma is already among its established indications, so this is confirmation of known use rather than true repurposing.
The data compiled for this report include **50 clinical trials** and **20 publications** for this indication.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, docetaxel is a taxane that stabilises microtubules and blocks cell division (mitosis). This is the basis of its cytotoxic activity in rapidly dividing tumour cells such as breast carcinoma cells.

Breast carcinoma and breast neoplasm are already listed among docetaxel's original indications. The high TxGNN score (0.999) is therefore consistent with known use. The evidence below is established-use evidence, not evidence for a new indication.

Other predicted indications are far weaker. Ewing sarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma have small Phase II or combination-regimen data only. The remaining predictions (well-differentiated fetal adenocarcinoma of the lung, small cell lung carcinoma, primary pulmonary lymphoma, pulmonary blastoma and several rhabdomyosarcoma subtypes) have no supporting efficacy evidence or only case reports, and are recommended as "Hold".

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications / conditions of concern (from knowledge-graph links, not label text):** liver disease, gallbladder disease, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, peripheral neuropathy and other peripheral nervous system diseases, anaemia, hyperbilirubinaemia (including Gilbert syndrome), diarrhoeal disease, pulmonary oedema, stomatitis, neuritis (including motor nerve neuritis) and cardiac tamponade.
- **Drug Interactions:** 100 interactions were identified. The main ones listed are:
  - **Major:** clarithromycin.
  - **Moderate:** aprepitant, rolapitant, cimetidine, metronidazole, tinidazole, miconazole, eliglustat, rosuvastatin, simvastatin and troglitazone.
  - **Minor:** dexamethasone, prednisolone, prednisone and levofloxacin.

Package insert warnings and contraindications have not been obtained. Please refer to the package insert for authoritative safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Docetaxel's use in breast carcinoma is supported by multiple large completed Phase 3 trials (several with thousands of patients), so the evidence is strong, and the drug is already marketed in Singapore under 12 registrations. This is established use, not new repurposing. Safety is not fully characterised from label text, and the drug carries high myelosuppression risk.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Singapore package insert warnings, contraindications and approved indication text for each registration
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- A safety monitoring plan covering myelosuppression, hepatic impairment, neuropathy and the major clarithromycin interaction
- For the other predicted indications (Ewing sarcoma, rhabdomyosarcoma, pulmonary tumours), indication-specific evidence before further consideration. Currently they remain "Research Question" or "Hold".
