Naloxone is a competitive mu-opioid receptor antagonist, generally used to reverse opioid effects. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **continuous spikes and waves during sleep** (an epileptic encephalopathy).

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Naloxone is known to block mu-opioid receptors competitively, and this is the basis of its established use against opioid effects.

Continuous spikes and waves during sleep is an epileptic encephalopathy. No opioid-related mechanism is described for it, and no established link exists between naloxone's pharmacology and this condition. The model score should be read as a computational signal only, not as mechanistic support.
