## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

No clinical, preclinical, or case-level literature in this evidence pack links naloxone or opioid antagonism to this syndrome.

This candidate should therefore be treated strictly as a knowledge-graph association (TxGNN score only) rather than a mechanistically or clinically supported hypothesis. The model's rationale field itself states there is no mechanistic basis for this pairing.
