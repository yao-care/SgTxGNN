# Abacavir: From HIV Infection / AIDS to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Abacavir is a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI) originally used to treat HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**, a virus that infects non-human primates.
Support is very thin: **0 clinical trials** and **1 publication**, an in vitro laboratory study.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Abacavir is known to be an NRTI active against HIV-1. It blocks the virus's reverse transcriptase, the enzyme the virus needs to copy its genetic material.

SIV is a close relative of HIV, a lentivirus with a similar reverse transcriptase. It is therefore biologically plausible that abacavir would also act against it, which is probably why the model gives such a high score.

SIV is a virus of non-human primates and is mainly used as an animal model for HIV research. It is not a human disease, so this prediction is better read as a research-model link than as a treatment opportunity for patients. There is no clinical development path for this indication.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only support is a 2004 laboratory study, with no clinical trials. SIV is an animal virus, so there is no realistic human development path for this indication.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator, which are currently missing
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- A clear research purpose for this indication, such as use only as a preclinical model, since no clinical use is expected

**Note:** Among the other candidates compiled for abacavir, **congenital HIV** has far stronger support (a completed Phase 3 trial and several cohort studies). It is an extension of the already approved HIV use and may be a better focus for further evaluation.
