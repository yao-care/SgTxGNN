## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on the published literature, alprostadil (PGE1) relaxes the smooth muscle of the ductus arteriosus. Infusion keeps the ductus open in newborns whose circulation depends on it, which can maintain pulmonary or systemic blood flow until surgery.

This is the rationale for using it in some congenital aortic arch lesions, such as coarctation or interrupted aortic arch. A 1982 clinical evaluation in infants with congenital heart disease and a case report of interrupted aortic arch managed with alprostadil before single-stage repair fit this picture. Its use here would be a bridging measure before surgery, not a treatment of the malformation itself.

Some studies also suggest secondary anti-inflammatory and organ-protective effects, for example after cardiopulmonary bypass in children with congenital heart disease. These findings are indirect and do not address aortic malformation specifically.

The evidence level is L5 because none of the cited publications is a study that directly addresses aortic malformation as the disease under study. The cited papers cover congenital heart disease in general, interrupted aortic arch in a single case, and other conditions.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication-related conditions** (from relation data, not label text): newborn and acute respiratory distress syndrome, hypotensive disorder, blood coagulation disease, sickle cell disease and other hemoglobinopathies, lung disease, plasma cell myeloma, leukaemia, polycythaemia, thrombocytosis, priapism, Peyronie disease, urethral stricture, balanitis and urethritis, and pyloric stenosis. These should be checked against the package insert.
- **Reported adverse effects with prolonged infusion in neonates** (case reports): gastric outlet obstruction (antral foveolar hyperplasia, hypertrophic pyloric stenosis) and pseudo-Bartter syndrome with electrolyte disturbance. Apnoea is a recognised effect and needs monitoring.
- **Drug Interactions**: 59 interacting drugs were identified. Moderate-level interactions include canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, ertugliflozin, lorcaserin and sapropterin. Others, such as morphine, metformin, omeprazole, pantoprazole, prednisone, simvastatin, vancomycin, amphotericin B and atropine, are of unknown severity.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available for this report; please refer to the package insert for authoritative safety information.
