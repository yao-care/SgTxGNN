# Pemetrexed: From Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer and Pleural Mesothelioma to Malignant Peritoneal Mesothelioma

## One-Sentence Summary

Pemetrexed is an antifolate chemotherapy originally used for non-small cell lung cancer and malignant pleural mesothelioma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **malignant peritoneal mesothelioma**,
with **7 clinical trials** and **20 publications** currently supporting this direction. Pemetrexed-platinum is already used for this disease by extrapolation from pleural disease, so this is closer to a label extension than a novel repurposing.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, pemetrexed is a multitargeted antifolate that inhibits thymidylate synthase, DHFR and GARFT. This disrupts folate-dependent nucleotide synthesis and slows tumour cell growth.

Its efficacy in malignant pleural mesothelioma is established. A Phase 3 RCT of pemetrexed plus cisplatin versus cisplatin alone showed a survival benefit and led to approval. Peritoneal mesothelioma shares the same histology and basic biology, so the mechanism is expected to carry over.

Peritoneal disease is typically treated with cytoreductive surgery plus hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy (HIPEC) when the patient is suitable. For advanced disease, pemetrexed-platinum is already the commonly used systemic regimen. However, peritoneal-specific prospective evidence is scarce, and recommendations are mostly extrapolated from pleural trials.

## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication-related conditions (knowledge-graph associations, not label text)**: kidney disease and impaired renal function, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, anaemia (including macrocytic anaemia), and pyoureter.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. The main ones shown are all moderate: NSAID-type and salicylate drugs (acetylsalicylic acid, mesalazine, balsalazide, olsalazine), H2 blockers (famotidine, ranitidine, cimetidine), metformin, amphotericin B formulations, aminoglycosides and polymyxin B (kanamycin, neomycin, polymyxin B), vancomycin, amoxicillin, levofloxacin and sapropterin. Many of these affect renal clearance or kidney function.

Please refer to the package insert for label warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Pemetrexed-platinum has Phase 3 evidence in pleural mesothelioma and is already the standard systemic backbone for peritoneal mesothelioma by extrapolation. Peritoneal-specific evidence is limited to small Phase 2 trials and retrospective series, so the evidence level is L2.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from HSA, which are currently missing and block formal safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation of the approved indication text for the Singapore licences
- Results from the ongoing randomized peritoneal trials (NCT05001880, NCT03875144, NCT06057935)
- Renal function and blood count monitoring plan, with vitamin supplementation and interaction review (especially NSAIDs and nephrotoxic drugs)
