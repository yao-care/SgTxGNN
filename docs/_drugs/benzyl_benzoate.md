## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, benzyl benzoate is a topical ectoparasiticide whose efficacy is directed at mites and lice on the skin. Mechanistically, there is no established basis for use in pharyngeal disease.

The prediction rests only on the model's score. Benzyl benzoate has no recognised antimicrobial role on the throat or other mucosal surfaces. It is a known skin and mucosal irritant that can cause contact dermatitis, so applying it to the oropharynx would be a safety concern rather than a therapeutic rationale.

The other nine top predictions are all mucosal or upper-airway conditions: nasal cavity disease, acute laryngopharyngitis, and several vaginal or vulvar conditions. All are L5, model-only predictions with no clinical support and the same irritation concern.
