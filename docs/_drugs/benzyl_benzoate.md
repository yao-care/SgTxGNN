## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Benzyl benzoate is a topical agent known to act on ectoparasites such as scabies mites and lice, probably through neurotoxic effects on the parasite. No plausible link to pharyngeal infection or inflammation has been established.

The predicted indication is therefore not supported by a known pharmacological rationale. The TxGNN score of 0.887 is a computational output only. Other top-ranked predictions include nasal cavity disease, vaginitis, acute laryngopharyngitis, vulvitis, bacterial vaginosis, vaginal discharge, vulvovaginitis, leukoplakia of vagina and vulvovaginal candidiasis. None of them has clinical or mechanistic support in the data compiled for this report. Benzyl benzoate is also a known skin and mucosal irritant and potential contact sensitizer, which is a concern for mucosal sites such as the throat, nose and genital tract.

## Safety Considerations

- **Known irritation potential**: Benzyl benzoate is a known skin and mucosal irritant and a potential contact sensitizer, so use on inflamed or mucosal tissue (throat, nasal cavity, vulvovaginal area) raises safety concerns.

No interaction records were found in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the package insert for further safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction for pharyngitis has no clinical trials, no literature and no mechanistic basis. The evidence level is L5, and the irritant nature of the drug argues against use on mucosal sites without further work. The product is registered in Singapore only as a topical emulsion, and its formulation is not suited to the predicted site.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
