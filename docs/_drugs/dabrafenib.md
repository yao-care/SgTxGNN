# Dabrafenib: From Metastatic Melanoma to Non-Cutaneous Melanoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Dabrafenib is a BRAF kinase inhibitor, originally used to treat metastatic melanoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **non-cutaneous melanoma** (mucosal, uveal, conjunctival and similar types), with **8 clinical trials** supporting this direction.
These trials are mostly early-phase, mixed-tumour or access/roll-over studies, so the evidence is moderate and no study isolates non-cutaneous melanoma.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not currently available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, dabrafenib is a BRAF V600 kinase inhibitor, usually combined with the MEK inhibitor trametinib. Its efficacy in BRAF V600-mutant metastatic melanoma is established.

A subset of non-cutaneous melanomas (mucosal, uveal, conjunctival and others) carry BRAF V600 mutations. This maps directly onto dabrafenib's target. The mutation is less frequent than in cutaneous melanoma, so molecular selection (BRAF V600 positivity) would be essential.

Related melanoma subtypes also show BRAF-directed activity in case-level reports. These include BRAF-mutated conjunctival (epithelioid) melanoma, scrotal melanoma and eyelid melanoma. They support plausibility but not efficacy.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Dabrafenib's BRAF-inhibition mechanism directly applies to BRAF V600-mutant non-cutaneous melanoma, and Phase 2 and early-phase studies, including a biomarker-driven basket trial, support the direction. However, no study isolates non-cutaneous melanoma efficacy, and the supporting literature is limited to case reports and reviews, so any further work should be restricted to BRAF V600-positive patients.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications for the Singapore licences (currently missing, and a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Non-cutaneous melanoma-specific results from NCT04439292 and NCT07440290 once available
- A BRAF V600 testing and patient-selection plan
- A review of the interacting drugs in this population
- Approved indication text for the Singapore licences, to confirm the original indication
