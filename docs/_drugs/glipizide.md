Not applicable. Glipizide is not an antineoplastic drug.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were found. Most listed ones are Moderate: beta-agonists and sympathomimetics (salbutamol, formoterol, epinephrine, phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine), acebutolol, H2 blockers (famotidine, ranitidine), acetazolamide, hydrochlorothiazide, NSAIDs (ibuprofen, ketorolac), ethinylestradiol, hydrocortisone, acitretin, albiglutide, nitazoxanide and ethanol. Topical corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, alclometasone) are Minor.

Please also refer to the package insert for safety information, as label warnings and contraindications are not available in the data compiled for this report.
