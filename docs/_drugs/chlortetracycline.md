## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available for this drug. Chlortetracycline belongs to the tetracycline class, which inhibits bacterial protein synthesis. Tetracyclines are also known to have anti-inflammatory effects, such as inhibition of matrix metalloproteinases and modulation of neutrophil function.

Postinfectious vasculitis is an inflammatory condition that follows an infection. A link through tetracycline-class anti-inflammatory activity is plausible, but it is unverified. No trials or publications were provided to confirm it, and the prediction should be treated as a hypothesis only.
