## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, chlortetracycline is a tetracycline-class antibiotic. Its efficacy in bacterial eye infections is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to postinfectious vasculitis.

A plausible but unverified link is the anti-inflammatory activity seen in this class, such as inhibition of matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) and modulation of neutrophil function. Postinfectious vasculitis is an immune-mediated inflammatory condition that follows an infection, so such effects could be relevant in principle. The data compiled for this report contain no trials or publications to confirm this.
