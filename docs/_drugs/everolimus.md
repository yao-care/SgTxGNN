# Everolimus: From Renal Cell Carcinoma to Liposarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Everolimus is an mTOR inhibitor, originally used for renal cell carcinoma, breast carcinoma, and tuberous sclerosis-related conditions.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**.
Support so far is **1 clinical trial** (a combination Phase 2 study) and **4 publications**, only one of which reports clinical trial data.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Everolimus is known to inhibit mTORC1, a central regulator of cell growth and proliferation. Its efficacy in renal cell carcinoma and other tumours has been established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to liposarcoma.

Dedifferentiated liposarcoma is typically driven by MDM2/CDK4 amplification, and it often shows activation of the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway. Blocking mTOR with everolimus while blocking CDK4/6 with ribociclib targets two complementary proliferation pathways. Preclinical models have shown synergistic growth inhibition from this combination.

The evidence is still early. The one directly relevant trial is a single-arm Phase 2 study of the combination. Its results are not established in the data compiled, and everolimus's own contribution cannot be separated from ribociclib's.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only directly relevant evidence is a single-arm Phase 2 combination trial that has not yet completed, so everolimus's own effect in liposarcoma cannot be established. The prediction score is very high, but the clinical evidence is early. The official label safety data have also not been reviewed.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Final results of NCT03114527, including response and progression-free survival in the dedifferentiated liposarcoma cohort
- Data that separate everolimus's contribution from ribociclib's, ideally a randomised comparison
- The HSA package insert warnings and contraindications, to enable formal safety screening
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- A safety and drug-interaction management plan, particularly for CYP3A4-related interactions, cytopenias, and metabolic effects
