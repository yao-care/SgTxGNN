# Darunavir: From HIV-1 Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Darunavir is an HIV-1 protease inhibitor, used in humans against HIV-1 infection.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **Simian Immunodeficiency Virus (SIV) Infection**, the macaque counterpart of HIV.
Support is limited: **0 clinical trials** and **4 preclinical publications** in macaques, none of which isolates darunavir's own effect.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, darunavir is an HIV-1 protease inhibitor, and its efficacy in HIV-1 infection is established. SIV is a closely related lentivirus with a structurally similar protease, so a class-level rationale exists for the prediction.

The prediction mainly reflects how closely the two diseases are related. SIV infection in macaques is the standard animal model of HIV. In practice, the high score most likely comes from the shared viral target rather than from any new clinical use.

The four supporting papers are macaque studies of combination antiretroviral therapy (cART). Some add other agents, such as an HDAC inhibitor or auranofin. From the titles and abstracts, darunavir's individual contribution cannot be separated from the rest of the regimen. This is indirect, preclinical evidence.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The evidence is limited to four macaque cART studies with no direct darunavir comparison, and there are no clinical trials. SIV infection is an animal model of a disease darunavir already treats in humans, so it has little value as a human indication. It is better treated as a research question than a development candidate.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Full-text review of the four macaque studies to confirm whether darunavir was part of each regimen and what it contributed
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator (HSA), which are needed for any safety screening
- Approved-indication text for the Singapore registrations, to confirm the human HIV-1 label
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank

Among the other predictions, "AIDS related complex" and "congenital human immunodeficiency virus" have the strongest trial support. Both are most likely covered by existing HIV use rather than new repurposing, so their labels should be checked first.
