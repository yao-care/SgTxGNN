## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, axitinib is a selective inhibitor of vascular endothelial growth factor receptors 1, 2 and 3 (VEGFR-1/2/3). Its efficacy in RCC is well established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to other kidney tumours that depend on tumour blood vessel growth.

The Singapore licence text for axitinib does not state an approved indication. The original indication above therefore comes from the TxGNN knowledge graph. The prediction most likely arises because this condition sits close to RCC in the knowledge graph. No trials or publications were found for this specific entity, so there is no independent mechanistic or clinical support for it. The relationship between the original and predicted indication should be treated as a statistical association, not a demonstrated pharmacological link.

For context, other predicted indications in the same analysis have more support. Two examples are TFE3-translocation RCC, with a randomised Phase 2 trial of axitinib plus nivolumab (NCT03595124, n=15), and collecting duct carcinoma, with a Phase 2 trial of checkpoint inhibitors plus axitinib (NCT06211114, n=30). Neither has results yet.
