# Arginine: From Approved Infusion Products to Gastroparesis

## One-Sentence Summary

Arginine is an amino acid marketed in Singapore in infusion products, but the registration records on file state no approved indication text.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **Gastroparesis**, but there is **1 clinical trial** (unrelated to arginine or gastroparesis) and **10 publications** (nearly all animal studies), so the evidence is preclinical only.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. The following rationale is based on the mechanistic link identified during evidence review.

L-arginine is the substrate of neuronal nitric oxide synthase (nNOS). nNOS produces the nitric oxide (NO) needed for nitrergic relaxation of the stomach, including gastric accommodation. Mouse and rat studies link impaired NO signalling to delayed gastric emptying. Examples are tetrahydrobiopterin deficiency, pyloric nitrergic dysfunction in a Parkinson's disease rat model, and glucocorticoid-induced arginine depletion in mice.

This makes a mechanistic case that supplying arginine could support gastric motor function. However, no human trial has tested arginine for gastroparesis. Arginine's original licensed indication is also not stated in the local records, so an "original-to-new indication" comparison cannot be made.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The 99.42% model score is supported only by animal studies and a plausible NO-pathway mechanism. The single retrieved trial is unrelated, and there is no human evidence for arginine in gastroparesis.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Singapore package insert warnings and contraindications (HSA), which are currently missing and block safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data (e.g. from DrugBank)
- Human data, starting with a small proof-of-concept study of arginine or citrulline in gastroparesis or functional dyspepsia (gastric emptying and accommodation endpoints)
- Route and formulation assessment, since only injectable and infusion products are registered locally
