## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Pharmacology data shows that methylnaltrexone binds the mu, delta and kappa opioid receptors. It is a peripherally restricted antagonist that does not appreciably cross the blood-brain barrier. This lets it relieve opioid-induced constipation without reversing central pain relief.

The predicted condition is a genetic disorder of hair follicle development. No known role for opioid receptor signalling exists in this process, so no plausible mechanistic link could be identified.
