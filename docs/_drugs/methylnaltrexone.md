## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Methylnaltrexone is generally described as a peripherally restricted mu-opioid receptor antagonist. It does not meaningfully cross the blood-brain barrier.

No mechanistic link to congenital hypotrichosis milia has been identified. This is a hereditary hair-follicle disorder with no known connection to peripheral opioid receptor blockade. The score is a model prediction only, and it should not be read as biological support.

The other top-ranked predictions show the same pattern. Most are hair-loss disorders (hypotrichosis, alopecia, alopecia areata), and none has trials or literature. This suggests the high scores may be knowledge-graph artifacts rather than real signals.
