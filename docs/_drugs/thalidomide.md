## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on the information on hand, the only plausible link is the anti-angiogenic and immunomodulatory activity of thalidomide seen in neuroblastoma, a related tumour. Direct data for ganglioneuroblastoma do not support this link.

The model score is high (rank 10,815), but a high score alone is not evidence. The neuroblastoma data come from a separate prediction for the same drug (L2 evidence). They include Phase 2 trials of multi-drug regimens containing thalidomide, a xenograft study showing anti-angiogenic activity, and preclinical work on cereblon (CRBN) biology. These studies do not address ganglioneuroblastoma directly, and the Phase 2 data are single-arm combination regimens that cannot isolate thalidomide's contribution.
