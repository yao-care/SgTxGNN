## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications flagged by the TxGNN model (deep learning)** (these come from the TxGNN model, not from label text, and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence): thrombocytopenia, thrombocytopenic purpura, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, inherited aplastic anemia, Diamond-Blackfan anemia, blood coagulation disease, active peptic ulcer disease, intracerebral hemorrhage, cholestasis (including benign recurrent and familial intrahepatic forms), hyperlipidemia (including familial), leukemia, and ascorbic acid deficiency/scurvy.
- **Haematological signal from the literature**: the retrieved papers note that ticlopidine carries a higher frequency of thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura and neutropenia than clopidogrel. This is a major safety consideration for any new long-term use.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified in total. The main moderate-level interactions in the first 20 listed are acetylsalicylic acid, cimetidine, bupropion, alosetron, eluxadoline, nitisinone, fenfluramine and dexfenfluramine. Minor interactions include corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, prednisone, prednisolone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, triamcinolone) and antacids (aluminium, magnesium and calcium salts).

Please also refer to the package insert for label warnings and contraindications, which were not available in the data compiled for this report.
