## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the record. Based on known pharmacology, lorazepam is a positive allosteric modulator of the GABA-A receptor. It is used mainly for anxiety, sedation and seizure control.

There is no credible mechanistic link between this action and tumour biology. Lorazepam has no known antitumour activity, and a benign or malignant growth of the trigeminal nerve is not a condition its mechanism would treat.

The very high score is probably explained by the drug's proximity in the knowledge graph to neuro-oncology symptom or seizure nodes, not by a direct therapeutic relationship. At most, lorazepam could play a supportive, symptom-control role in these patients (for example, for seizures or anxiety). That would not be a disease-modifying use.

**Other predicted indications:**
