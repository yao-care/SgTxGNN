The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **colobomatous microphthalmia-rhizomelic dysplasia syndrome**, a rare developmental and skeletal disorder.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Infliximab is a chimeric monoclonal antibody against TNF-alpha. Its efficacy in Crohn's disease and other immune-mediated inflammatory diseases is well established.

The analysis found **no identifiable link** between TNF-alpha blockade and this rare developmental and skeletal dysplasia. The condition is a congenital malformation syndrome, not a TNF-driven inflammatory disease. The high TxGNN score (90.22%) is a graph-based prediction only, and no trials or literature support it.

The prediction should therefore be treated as a model output that has not been validated. It is not a credible repurposing lead at this stage.
