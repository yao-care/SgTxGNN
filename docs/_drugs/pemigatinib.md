## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Pemigatinib is a selective inhibitor of FGFR1-3 (fibroblast growth factor receptors). Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank is not available for this report, and the original indication could not be confirmed from the Singapore licence records.

The link to the predicted indication is weak. Multiple endocrine neoplasia syndromes are driven mainly by MEN1 loss-of-function and RET gain-of-function mutations, not FGFR signalling. The score (0.9971) comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence. The prediction is therefore not supported by known biology.

Other predicted indications were also reviewed. Their TxGNN scores are all similarly high (0.9925 to 0.9954), but none has clinical trial evidence:

- **HER2-positive breast carcinoma** is the only one with a biologically plausible rationale, because FGFR signalling can contribute to resistance to HER2-targeted therapy. The one linked publication is a general review of kinase inhibitors, not evidence specific to pemigatinib.
- **Amenorrhoea, cytomegalovirus infection, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (and related entries) and axial spondylometaphyseal dysplasia** have no plausible FGFR-inhibition mechanism.
- **Infectious bovine rhinotracheitis and malignant catarrh** are veterinary diseases with no human relevance. Their scores come from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence.
