## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications flagged in the knowledge graph** (not taken from the package insert): hypertension, liver failure and liver disease, kidney disease, gallbladder disease, epilepsy, porphyria, diabetes mellitus and hyperglycaemia, congestive heart failure, hypothyroidism, adrenocortical insufficiency (Addison disease), systemic lupus erythematosus, asthma, anxiety and related disorders, and humoral hypercalcaemia of malignancy. Osteoporosis and postmenopausal osteoporosis also appear in this list even though they are an original indication, so these links should be checked against the label.
- **Drug interactions**: 100 interactions were recorded, mostly of moderate severity. Examples include:
  - Antidiabetic agents: metformin, pioglitazone, acarbose, alogliptin, canagliflozin and dapagliflozin.
  - Corticosteroids: hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, betamethasone and budesonide.
  - Macrolide and other anti-infective agents: clarithromycin and clotrimazole.
  - Other: aprepitant. Cimetidine was classed as a minor interaction.

Please also refer to the package insert for full safety information, as no label warnings or contraindications were available.
