# Estradiol: From Approved Use (Indication Not Recorded) to Symptomatic Fragile X Syndrome in Female Carriers

## One-Sentence Summary

Estradiol is an estrogen that is registered and marketed in Singapore as gel, oral tablet, and vaginal tablet products. The TxGNN model predicts it may help with the **symptomatic form of fragile X syndrome in female carriers**. **No clinical trials and no publications** currently support this prediction, so it rests on the model score alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Estradiol is the principal natural estrogen, and its role in replacing estrogen is well established. Its approved indication text was not recorded in the Singapore registrations supplied.

One plausible link is that female carriers of the FMR1 premutation can develop ovarian insufficiency, a state of low estrogen where estrogen replacement is standard care. This is a hypothesis from general knowledge and is not supported by any data in this evidence pack. The model's proximity score may also reflect graph structure rather than a real therapeutic relationship, and the many similar low-evidence chromosomal-syndrome predictions for this drug suggest that risk.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has a high model score but no trials, no literature, and no confirmed mechanism, so it stays at evidence level L5. Safety information is also missing, which prevents any safety screening.

By comparison, the second-ranked prediction for this drug (anovulation) has trial and literature activity. Even there, estradiol appears mostly as an adjunct, and no Phase 2/3 RCT tests it directly.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Singapore package insert warnings and contraindications
- Mechanism of action data
- A targeted literature and trial search on estradiol in FMR1 premutation carriers, including premutation-associated ovarian insufficiency
- Assessment of which route of administration would be needed for this indication
