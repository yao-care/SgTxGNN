## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction query completed with 4 records. These are pharmacological target associations rather than graded drug-drug interactions, and no severity levels are assigned. They link piracetam to the AMPA receptor subunits GluA1, GluA2, GluA3 and GluA4 (genes GRIA1–GRIA4).

For other safety information, including warnings and contraindications, please refer to the package insert.
