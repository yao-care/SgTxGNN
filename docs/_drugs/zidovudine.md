## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Zidovudine is, however, a well-known nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI). Its relation to HIV infection and AIDS is recorded in the knowledge graph.

Simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) is a lentivirus closely related to HIV and replicates through the same reverse transcriptase-dependent cycle. This makes the mechanistic link strong. In macaque studies, immediate zidovudine treatment protected SIV-infected newborn macaques against rapid onset of AIDS. Post-exposure prophylaxis, in combination with lamivudine and indinavir, prevented vaginal SIV transmission. Treatment also prolonged survival and reduced central nervous system viral load. A zidovudine-resistant SIV mutant (Q151M) has also been described, which supports on-target activity.

SIV infection is a research model in animals, not a human clinical indication. The data therefore mainly validate the model and the drug's existing antiretroviral activity. They do not open a new human use.

A second animal-model prediction, feline acquired immunodeficiency syndrome (FIV and FeLV in cats), has similar veterinary and preclinical support (L5 under the rule that no human-disease study exists). Two further predictions have no supporting studies:
- A rare neurodevelopmental disorder with ataxic gait, absent speech and decreased cortical white matter.
- An obsolete ontology term for familial combined hyperlipidemia.

Neither has a plausible mechanism. NRTIs are associated with mitochondrial and lipid disturbances, so benefit is unlikely.

## Safety Considerations

No label warnings or contraindications are available in the data compiled for this report. Please refer to the package insert.

- **Knowledge-graph contraindication relations (not label text)**: liver failure, liver disease, fatty liver disease, non-alcoholic steatohepatitis, kidney disease, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, pyoureter, myopathy and other muscle diseases (including myositis and dermatomyositis, familial periodic paralysis), neutropenia, agranulocytosis, anaemia (including macrocytic anaemia), hypertriglyceridemia, obesity disorders, and lactic acidosis.
- **Drug Interactions** (100 interactions found; main ones shown):
  - Moderate: amphotericin B (including cholesteryl sulfate and lipid complex forms), corticosteroids (betamethasone, budesonide, dexamethasone, hydrocortisone, prednisolone, prednisone, triamcinolone), naltrexone, rosuvastatin, simvastatin, orlistat.
  - Minor: cimetidine, clarithromycin.
  - Unknown severity: pantoprazole, morphine, metformin, omeprazole.
- **Animal toxicity**: zidovudine caused hematologic toxicity in FeLV-infected cats, a caution for cross-species interpretation.
