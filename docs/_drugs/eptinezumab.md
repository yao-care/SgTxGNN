# Eptinezumab: From Migraine Prevention to Migraine with Brainstem Aura

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the input. Based on general knowledge, eptinezumab is a monoclonal antibody that neutralises calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP). CGRP is a potent cerebral vasodilator and a central part of the trigeminal migraine cascade.

Migraine with brainstem aura is a subtype of migraine with aura, so the mechanistic plausibility is high. Some indirect support exists:
- A post hoc analysis of the PROMISE-1 and PROMISE-2 trials evaluated eptinezumab in patients with self-reported aura.
- Case reports and a literature review discuss whether anti-CGRP antibodies help in migraine aura, though evidence on their effect on aura itself is scarce.

There are also limits on certainty:
- No study specific to brainstem aura was found.
- A randomized trial suggests PACAP38-induced migraine attacks are independent of CGRP signalling, so some attacks may not respond to CGRP blockade.
