## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, lacidipine is a dihydropyridine L-type calcium channel blocker that relaxes blood vessels and has vascular selectivity. Its efficacy as a class in vascular conditions is well established, so it may be mechanistically applicable to migraine.

Calcium channel modulation is biologically plausible in migraine. Another calcium channel blocker, flunarizine, is used for migraine prophylaxis. However, no trials or literature on lacidipine in migraine were retrieved, so the prediction currently reflects a class-level hypothesis, not lacidipine-specific proof.
