## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. From general pharmacology, fluorouracil is a thymidylate synthase inhibitor and antimetabolite. It disrupts DNA and RNA synthesis in rapidly dividing cells, which is a plausible rationale for a fast-growing sarcoma.

The relationship to the recorded uses is loose. Fluorouracil is used for epithelial and gastrointestinal cancers, whereas embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma is a soft-tissue sarcoma of childhood and young adulthood. Standard rhabdomyosarcoma regimens (vincristine, actinomycin D, cyclophosphamide-based) do not use 5-FU as a core agent. The prediction therefore reflects a generic antiproliferative rationale, not a subtype-specific biological link. The score comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence.

The same pattern holds for other sarcoma-related predictions in this set. Rhabdomyosarcoma in general has only indirect or anecdotal literature, and the other related subtypes (parameningeal, extrahepatic bile duct, prostate) have no direct studies. Predictions for sickle cell syndromes are speculative and unsupported for this drug.
