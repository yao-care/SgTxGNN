# Lenvatinib: From Established Anticancer Use to Liposarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Lenvatinib is an oral multi-kinase inhibitor already used in cancer treatment, and it is marketed in Singapore as capsules.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**, but the support is thin: **1 clinical trial** (single-arm, lenvatinib combined with eribulin) and **4 publications**, of which only one reports clinical data.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank is not available. The Evidence Pack describes lenvatinib as a multi-kinase inhibitor acting on VEGFR1-3, FGFR1-4, PDGFRα, RET and KIT. Its anti-angiogenic activity may complement eribulin, a chemotherapy drug that disrupts cell division (mitosis) by targeting microtubules, in adipocytic sarcoma.

Preclinical work supports this direction. Eribulin shows synergy when combined with mechanistically different anticancer agents, and soft tissue sarcoma (including dedifferentiated liposarcoma) shows a CDK4 dependency. The main limitation is that the only clinical data are single-arm and test the combination, so lenvatinib's own contribution cannot be separated from eribulin's.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Liposarcoma is supported by one small, non-randomized Phase Ib/II study of lenvatinib combined with eribulin, plus preclinical work. This is consistent with the Evidence Pack's own label of "Research Question". Lenvatinib's independent effect is unproven, and the safety data needed for screening are missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- HSA package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Randomized or lenvatinib-controlled evidence in liposarcoma, and the reported efficacy results from the LEADER study
- A review of other predicted indications. The Evidence Pack lists renal carcinoma (rank 7) with L1 evidence, but it is already an established lenvatinib use and so is not a true repurposing case.
