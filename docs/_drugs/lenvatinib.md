# Lenvatinib: From Renal Cell Carcinoma and Thyroid Cancer to Liposarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Lenvatinib is an oral multi-targeted kinase inhibitor, originally used for renal cell carcinoma and thyroid cancer.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**.
Support is limited: **1 completed early-phase clinical trial** (a combination with eribulin) and **4 publications**, only one of which reports clinical results in patients.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data for lenvatinib is not available in the data compiled for this report. The following is based on general knowledge of the drug. Lenvatinib blocks several growth-signalling receptors: VEGFR1-3, FGFR1-4, PDGFRα, RET and KIT. By doing so it cuts off the blood supply that tumours need and interferes with their proliferative signalling. This anti-angiogenic action is the basis of its use in renal cell and thyroid cancers.

Liposarcoma is a soft tissue sarcoma that also depends on new blood vessel growth. Lenvatinib has been tested with eribulin, a microtubule-targeting chemotherapy already used in advanced liposarcoma. The two drugs act through different mechanisms, so the combination is plausible. Separately, CDK4 amplification is common in well-differentiated and dedifferentiated liposarcoma, and CDK4 inhibition is being studied as a synergistic approach.

The link is therefore reasonable but indirect. The evidence comes from one small single-arm study of a combination, so it does not show that lenvatinib alone is effective.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
Support for liposarcoma comes from a single small, single-arm Phase 1b/2 study of lenvatinib combined with eribulin (n=30), so the contribution of lenvatinib alone is unclear. The package-insert safety information is also missing, which blocks safety screening. The drug is already marketed in Singapore, so access is not the barrier. The evidence is the barrier.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Complete results and a detailed efficacy readout (response rate, progression-free survival) from the LEADER study, ideally by liposarcoma subtype.
- Randomized or larger controlled data comparing the combination with standard therapy such as eribulin alone.
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator (HSA).
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data for lenvatinib.
- A plan for managing drug interactions and combination toxicity, especially with eribulin.
