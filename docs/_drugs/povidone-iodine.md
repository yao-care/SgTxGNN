## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Povidone-iodine is a topical iodine-releasing antiseptic that kills bacteria, fungi and viruses on skin and mucosa, and it is not known to act systemically.

No direct mechanistic link to malaria has been identified. The high score (rank 36,416 in the model's output) comes from the knowledge graph, not from any antiplasmodial data. Malaria is a systemic blood-stage parasitic infection. A topical antiseptic would not reach the parasite in the bloodstream.
