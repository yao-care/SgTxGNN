## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: Seven interaction records were found. Major interactions were reported with:
  - Anti-inhibitor coagulant complex
  - Tretinoin
  - Factor IX Complex (Human)

  Moderate interactions were reported with the thrombolytics alteplase, reteplase and tenecteplase. A pharmacology record also lists plasminogen as a target.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications. Note that aprotinin's systemic use carries renal and thrombotic safety concerns that would need to be considered for any new indication.
