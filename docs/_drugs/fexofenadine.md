# Fexofenadine: From Allergic Rhinitis to Rosacea Conjunctivitis

## One-Sentence Summary

Fexofenadine is a second-generation H1-antihistamine, originally used for allergic rhinitis, allergic and other forms of conjunctivitis, and idiopathic urticaria.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **rosacea conjunctivitis**,
but **no clinical trials and no publications** currently support this direction, so it is a model prediction only.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, fexofenadine is a selective peripheral H1-receptor antagonist. It blocks histamine-mediated itching, redness and swelling, and it is already used for allergic and atopic conjunctivitis.

A histamine-driven component could plausibly contribute to ocular surface inflammation in rosacea, which is the basis for the prediction. However, rosacea conjunctivitis is mainly driven by meibomian gland dysfunction, microbial and Demodex factors, and innate immune and neurovascular dysregulation. H1 blockade does not address these causes.

The high score most likely reflects the closeness of rosacea conjunctivitis to other conjunctivitis indications in the knowledge graph. It is not supported by clinical data. At most, a symptomatic benefit (itch and redness) is conceivable.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on model score alone (L5). No trials or publications exist, and the underlying pathology of rosacea conjunctivitis is not histamine-driven. The oral formulations registered in Singapore also do not match the likely ocular-surface need.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A focused literature review of antihistamines in rosacea-related ocular disease and, as a more plausible lead, eyelid dermatitis
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the HSA, to complete safety screening
- Assessment of route compatibility (oral versus topical ocular delivery)
- Specific indication text for the Singapore registrations
