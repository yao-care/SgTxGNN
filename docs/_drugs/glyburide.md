## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the record. Glyburide is a KATP channel (SUR1) blocker that triggers insulin secretion from pancreatic beta cells. Its efficacy is established in diabetes, but that mechanism has no known connection to opsismodysplasia.

Opsismodysplasia is a skeletal dysplasia caused by INPPL1 mutations. It involves a bone and cartilage development defect, not a glucose or insulin problem. We could not identify a plausible biological link. The high score (0.972) most likely reflects network proximity in the knowledge graph rather than a real therapeutic relationship.
