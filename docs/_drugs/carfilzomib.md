## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Carfilzomib belongs to the proteasome inhibitor class. By blocking protein degradation, these drugs cause proteotoxic stress in cells and can trigger apoptosis.

A plausible link is that this mechanism could act on melanoma-related cells, since the predicted disease is a melanoma-related condition. However, no trial, publication or mechanistic study was retrieved for CMM7 itself, so this link is an assumption and not supported by data.
