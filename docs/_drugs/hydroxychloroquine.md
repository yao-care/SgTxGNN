## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, hydroxychloroquine is an antimalarial with immunomodulatory effects. Its efficacy in rheumatoid arthritis and lupus is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to inflammatory skin and hair disorders.

Folliculitis decalvans is a chronic, relapsing neutrophilic scarring alopecia with a dysregulated inflammatory component. Hydroxychloroquine is thought to inhibit Toll-like receptor signalling, reduce antigen presentation and lower pro-inflammatory cytokine production. These effects plausibly support its use as an adjuvant. A 5-year retrospective study of 49 patients suggests possible benefit, and the EADV task force statement addresses management of the condition. The evidence is limited to retrospective data and expert guidance, with no prospective trials.

Other predicted indications in the model output were also reviewed. Among them, **alopecia areata** has the most clinical data (one small Phase 4 open-label study and several case series, with conflicting results). **Follicular mucinosis** has several supportive case reports. The remaining predictions (telogen effluvium, rare genetic syndromes, rheumatoid nodulosis and others) have little or no supporting evidence.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high, but the evidence for folliculitis decalvans is limited to one retrospective cohort and an expert statement, with no registered trials. In addition, the safety data (package insert warnings and contraindications) are incomplete, which prevents safety screening. The case is better treated as a research question than as a candidate for immediate action.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from HSA (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data (for example from DrugBank)
- Prospective or controlled data for HCQ in folliculitis decalvans
- Eye safety (retinal toxicity) monitoring plan, given the eye-disease flags and long-term use in a chronic condition
- Review of alopecia areata and follicular mucinosis as alternative lead indications, given their larger bodies of clinical evidence
