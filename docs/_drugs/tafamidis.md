# Tafamidis: From Transthyretin Amyloidosis to Primary Release Disorder of Platelets

## One-Sentence Summary

Tafamidis is an oral transthyretin (TTR) stabilizer used in transthyretin amyloidosis, and the Singapore registration lists it as VYNDAMAX 61 mg capsules.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Tafamidis binds the thyroxine-binding sites of the TTR tetramer and kinetically stabilizes it. This stops the tetramer from dissociating into amyloidogenic monomers, which is the rate-limiting step of amyloid formation in TTR amyloidosis.

This mechanism does not plausibly connect to the predicted indication. Primary release disorder of platelets is a defect in platelet secretion, and TTR has no established role in platelet release or secretion. The score of 89.27% reflects graph-based proximity in the TxGNN knowledge graph, not biological or clinical evidence. The same pattern appears across the other bleeding and platelet-related predictions for this drug (thrombocytopenic purpura, pseudo-von Willebrand disease, Glanzmann thrombasthenia), none of which has trials or literature behind it.

The prediction rests only on a model score, with no trials, no publications, and no plausible mechanism linking TTR stabilization to platelet release. It should not advance.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
