# Brentuximab Vedotin: From Hodgkin Lymphoma to Follicular Lymphoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Brentuximab vedotin is an anti-CD30 antibody-drug conjugate originally used for Hodgkin lymphoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **follicular lymphoma**, but the supporting evidence is thin and indirect: **6 clinical trials** (3 withdrawn or terminated early, 1 small ongoing Phase 2) and **20 publications**, most of which concern other lymphomas.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed DrugBank mechanism-of-action data are not available. From the data compiled for this report, brentuximab vedotin is an anti-CD30 antibody-drug conjugate that delivers the cytotoxic payload MMAE to CD30-positive cells. This is why it works in Hodgkin lymphoma, where CD30 is consistently expressed.

Follicular lymphoma is a B-cell lymphoma, so it belongs to the same broad disease family as Hodgkin lymphoma. However, follicular lymphoma is usually CD30-low or CD30-negative, so the mechanistic rationale is weak. Any benefit would depend on CD30 expression in a subset of tumours, or on a "bystander effect" in which released MMAE kills neighbouring CD30-low cells. No validated target-expression link has been established.

The high graph score therefore reflects network proximity to lymphoma indications rather than a demonstrated biological mechanism in follicular lymphoma.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only follicular-lymphoma-specific trial is a small, ongoing Phase 2 study with no results. Three of the six linked trials were withdrawn with no patients, and no publication shows efficacy in follicular lymphoma. CD30 is usually low or absent in this disease, so the mechanistic basis is weak. The high TxGNN score alone does not justify advancing this indication.

For comparison, **B-cell neoplasm** (the fourth-ranked prediction, mainly CD30-positive DLBCL) has stronger support. It has a completed Phase 2 trial in 176 patients, a randomised Phase 3 trial in relapsed DLBCL that is no longer recruiting (239 patients), and a 2025 *J Clin Oncol* report of a brentuximab vedotin + lenalidomide + rituximab combination. It could be evaluated as a more promising direction.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Results from NCT04587687 (brentuximab vedotin + bendamustine in relapsed/refractory follicular lymphoma)
- Data on CD30 expression in follicular lymphoma, to define a biomarker-selected population
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore registration, which are currently missing
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation of the approved indication text for the Singapore registration
