## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Finasteride inhibits 5-alpha-reductase (type II/III). This lowers dihydrotestosterone (DHT), the hormone that drives hair-follicle miniaturisation in androgenetic alopecia and prostate enlargement in BPH. A detailed mechanism-of-action record is not available in this dataset, so the description above comes from the analysis attached to the prediction.

The link to the predicted indication is weak. Ambras syndrome is a congenital condition caused by structural variants affecting the regulation of the *TRPS1* gene. It is not driven by androgens, so blocking DHT has no clear reason to help. The score of about 0.9999 appears to reflect the model's closeness between finasteride and other hair-related disease terms. It is not evidence that the drug works here.

## Conclusion and Next Steps
