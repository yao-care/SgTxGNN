## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the source record. Dalteparin is a low-molecular-weight heparin with anti-Xa/IIa activity, so it is biologically plausible for a prothrombotic state. Severe protein C deficiency causes a strongly prothrombotic condition, and anticoagulation is conceptually relevant to it.

However, the score is model-predicted only, and no trials or literature were supplied to test the link. Severe protein C deficiency is a specialist setting where protein C replacement is the mainstay of treatment. Clinical relevance of dalteparin therefore cannot be judged from this data.
