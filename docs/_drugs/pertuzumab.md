# Pertuzumab: From HER2-Positive Breast Cancer to Progesterone-Receptor Positive Breast Cancer

## One-Sentence Summary

Pertuzumab is a HER2-targeted antibody originally used to treat HER2-positive breast cancer.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **progesterone-receptor positive breast cancer**, which is essentially a hormone-receptor subset of the already-treated disease.
Currently, **1 clinical trial** and **20 publications** support this direction, including several randomised neoadjuvant studies.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, pertuzumab binds the extracellular dimerisation domain of HER2 and blocks HER2/HER3 heterodimerisation. This dampens downstream PI3K/AKT signalling.

Many HER2-positive tumours also express progesterone receptor (the HR+/HER2+ subtype). In these tumours the HER2-driven mechanism is the same as in the approved indication. The prediction is therefore better seen as a receptor-defined subset of the labelled disease than as a true repurposing. The trials and publications are driven by HER2 status, and PR status is a co-existing tumour characteristic.

Endocrine-therapy combinations are being explored in this subgroup. Examples are the WSG TP-II trial (trastuzumab + pertuzumab + endocrine therapy versus chemotherapy) and the PERTAIN study (pertuzumab + trastuzumab + aromatase inhibitor).

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
PR-positive breast cancer is largely a receptor-defined subset of the approved HER2-positive indication, and the mechanism is the same. Randomised studies (e.g., WSG TP-II, NeoSphere) include HR+/HER2+ patients. The only registered trial listed is a biosimilar study in ER/PR-negative patients, which is indirect evidence for the PR-positive subgroup.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications (HSA label), which is a blocking gap for safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data (DrugBank)
- Approved indication text for the Singapore registrations, to confirm how the current labels define the HER2-positive population
- Subgroup efficacy data by PR status, and confirmation of HER2 positivity in the target patients
- A cardiac safety monitoring plan (LVEF), particularly with anthracycline-containing regimens
