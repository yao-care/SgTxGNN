# Isoleucine: From Parenteral Nutrition Amino Acid to Gastroparesis

## One-Sentence Summary

Isoleucine is an essential branched-chain amino acid, used in Singapore as a component of parenteral (intravenous) nutrition and amino acid infusion products.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **gastroparesis**, but **no clinical trials and no supporting publications** were found for this prediction, so it rests on model output alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, isoleucine is a branched-chain amino acid supplied as a component of amino acid infusions and combined parenteral nutrition products. Its established role is nutritional support, not treatment of a specific disease.

Amino acids can influence gastric motility and accommodation, so a link to gastroparesis is conceivable. However, the data compiled for this report contain no trials or publications supporting this, and no direct link to gastroparesis is established. The high score should therefore be read as a hypothesis generated by the model, not as evidence of efficacy.

Other predictions in the top 10 (for example dyspepsia, sclerosing cholangitis, renal tubular acidosis, potassium deficiency) are also supported only by indirect or tangential literature, and several (obsolete vitamin D deficiency, congenital prothrombin deficiency, hypophosphatemic rickets) have no plausible mechanistic link in the data.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The gastroparesis prediction has a high model score but no clinical trials, no literature, and no supported mechanism (evidence level L5). Isoleucine is also used as a nutritional component rather than as a stand-alone therapy, and the knowledge graph flags renal and cardiac conditions of concern.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from HSA, which are currently missing and block safety screening
- Mechanism of action data (for example from DrugBank)
- Approved indication text for the Singapore registrations
- A systematic literature search on branched-chain amino acids and gastric emptying or motility in gastroparesis
- Preclinical or early clinical studies to test the hypothesis
- Assessment of route and formulation compatibility, since current products are intravenous nutrition formulations
