# Naratriptan: From Acute Migraine to Migraine with Brainstem Aura

## One-Sentence Summary

Naratriptan is a triptan (5-HT1B/1D agonist) marketed for acute migraine.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **migraine with brainstem aura**, but **no clinical trials** and **no publications specific to this subtype** currently support the prediction. The 18 publications found cover migraine in general.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Naratriptan belongs to the triptan class of 5-HT1B/1D receptor agonists. This class is understood to act through cranial vasoconstriction and inhibition of CGRP release and trigeminal neurotransmission. This mechanism is plausible for any migraine type.

The prediction is weaker than its score suggests. The supporting literature covers migraine in general, including naratriptan RCTs in recurrence-prone migraine and in prodrome treatment, plus reviews. None of it tests the brainstem aura subtype. The very high score most likely reflects naratriptan's known migraine indication rather than a new repurposing signal.

There are also safety concerns:
- Triptan labelling usually lists basilar-type (brainstem aura) and hemiplegic migraine as contraindications because of ischaemic concerns.
- One cohort study (Neurology, 2015) suggests sumatriptan is less effective in migraine with aura than without aura.

The other nine predicted indications (for example amenorrhoea, sciatic neuropathy, dysthymic disorder and several rare hair and skin disorders) rest on graph-based scores only. They have no trials or literature and no plausible mechanistic link.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has no trials and no subtype-specific literature. The only supporting evidence is general migraine data, which places it at L4. Triptans are usually contraindicated in brainstem aura (basilar-type) migraine because of ischaemic concerns, so this is a potential safety conflict rather than a promising opportunity.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- The Singapore package insert (warnings and contraindications) from the HSA website, to confirm the labelled status of brainstem aura migraine
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Specialist neurology review of the ischaemic-risk concern
- Any trial or observational data in brainstem aura migraine before the decision is reconsidered
