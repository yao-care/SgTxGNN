## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Pharmacology records show that flupentixol binds dopamine D1, D2, D3 and D5 receptors. It is a thioxanthene antipsychotic whose effect in schizophrenia is understood to come mainly from dopamine receptor antagonism.

For the predicted indication, no mechanistic link is established. Inherited retinal dystrophies are genetically heterogeneous, and no known pathway connects dopamine receptor blockade to their underlying causes. The very high TxGNN score (0.9999) comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases, not biological evidence. It should not be read as evidence of efficacy.
