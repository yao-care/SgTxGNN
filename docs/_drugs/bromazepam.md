## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the Evidence Pack. Based on the supplied literature, bromazepam is a benzodiazepine that enhances GABA-A receptor-mediated inhibition. Its efficacy in anxiety is well documented.

A mechanistic link to migraine is possible but unproven. Enhanced GABAergic inhibition might reduce cortical hyperexcitability, or ease anxiety that co-occurs with migraine. No study in the pack tests this.

Benzodiazepines are not recommended for migraine prevention or acute treatment. Overuse and dependence are also a particular concern in headache patients. The high TxGNN score therefore looks like a knowledge-graph association rather than a clinically supported one.
