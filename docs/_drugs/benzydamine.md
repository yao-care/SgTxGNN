## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the record. Based on known pharmacology, benzydamine acts locally as an anti-inflammatory and local anesthetic, and it suppresses inflammatory cytokines such as TNF-alpha and IL-1beta.

The only conceivable link to BPH is that prostatic inflammation is thought to contribute to BPH, so a cytokine-suppressing agent could in theory matter. This link is speculative. Benzydamine is used almost entirely by local mucosal or topical application, and no route of delivery to the prostate has been shown. Neither trials nor literature support the prediction. The high score most likely reflects proximity in the knowledge graph rather than a demonstrated mechanism.
