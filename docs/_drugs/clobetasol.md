## One-Sentence Summary

Clobetasol is a super-potent topical glucocorticoid marketed in Singapore as creams, ointments and a scalp lotion.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **primary cutaneous T-cell lymphoma**, mainly early-stage mycosis fungoides.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Clobetasol is a super-potent topical glucocorticoid. Glucocorticoid receptor activation can induce apoptosis of malignant T cells and suppress local inflammatory and cytokine signalling in skin lesions.

This is a plausible mechanism for patch- and plaque-stage mycosis fungoides, the most common form of primary cutaneous T-cell lymphoma. Topical corticosteroids are already used in clinical practice for early-stage disease. The evidence applies only to skin-directed control of early-stage disease, not to systemic or advanced disease.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions are listed in the interaction database, but none has a severity grade. Examples include other corticosteroids (prednisone, prednisolone, hydrocortisone, triamcinolone), calcitriol, vitamin A, proton pump inhibitors (omeprazole, pantoprazole, lansoprazole), doxycycline, metronidazole and morphine.
- **Observed local effects**: In early-stage mycosis fungoides, prolonged use of clobetasol raises concern about corticosteroid-induced skin adverse effects (such as thinning), which was the subject of the 2020 observational study.

Please refer to the package insert for full warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Cohort data and case reports support topical clobetasol for early-stage mycosis fungoides, but no registered trial or randomised study exists, and evidence does not extend to advanced or systemic disease. The use is already part of dermatology practice, so the focus should be on controlled, skin-limited use.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore regulator
- Mechanism of action data
- Prospective or randomised data in early-stage disease
- A monitoring plan for long-term skin adverse effects, with specialist confirmation of diagnosis and staging before use
