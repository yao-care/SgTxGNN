## Paraldehyde: From Sedative-Hypnotic Use to Irritable Bowel Syndrome
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Paraldehyde is a legacy sedative-hypnotic and anticonvulsant, registered in Singapore as an injection. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **irritable bowel syndrome**, but **no clinical trials and no publications** currently support this prediction, so it rests on a computational score alone.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Paraldehyde is known as a sedative-hypnotic and anticonvulsant with central nervous system (CNS) depressant activity, probably through GABA-like effects, but this has not been confirmed in the data compiled for this report.

A possible link to irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) would run through central modulation of the gut-brain axis or reduced visceral hypersensitivity. This is speculative and has no supporting studies. Paraldehyde's poor tolerability (strong odor, tissue irritation, and a problematic toxic metabolite profile) also works against it.

The high score (99.97%) is a computational prediction only and should not be read as evidence of efficacy.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction for irritable bowel syndrome is supported only by a model score, with no trials or publications. The mechanism is speculative, and paraldehyde's tolerability and interaction profile are unfavorable. Safer, better-characterized options exist for the predicted indications.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Singapore HSA (currently blocking any safety screening)
- Mechanism of action data (e.g., from DrugBank) to support or refute a gut-brain or visceral hypersensitivity link
- Preclinical or early clinical evidence for irritable bowel syndrome
- Remapping of the obsolete neurogenic bladder term to a current disease concept, if that indication is to be revisited
- Confirmation of the original approved indication, which is blank in the registration record
