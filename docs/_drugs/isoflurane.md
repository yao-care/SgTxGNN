## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, isoflurane is a volatile (inhaled) anaesthetic. Its efficacy as a general anaesthetic is established, and mechanistically it may have some relevance to Prinzmetal angina.

Prinzmetal angina is caused by coronary artery vasospasm. Volatile anaesthetics relax vascular smooth muscle and modulate calcium channels, which could plausibly counter vasospasm. This link is theoretical only. Isoflurane is also associated with coronary steal concerns, and it has no pathway for chronic use. It is a hospital-administered anaesthetic, not a maintenance therapy for a recurrent condition.
