## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available for this report. Azathioprine is known as a purine-antimetabolite immunosuppressant. It suppresses the proliferation of immune cells, which is why it is used in immune-mediated conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis.

The predicted condition is a rare developmental syndrome affecting the eye and skeleton. It has no known inflammatory or immune-mediated driver that azathioprine would plausibly address. No mechanistic link could be identified between the original indication and this new one.

The very high score is a graph-based prediction only. It should not be read as evidence of clinical benefit.
