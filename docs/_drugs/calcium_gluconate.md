# Calcium Gluconate: From Calcium Replacement to Calcium-Alkali Syndrome

Calcium gluconate is a calcium salt marketed in Singapore as an injection and as a hydrofluoric acid antidote gel. ## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Calcium gluconate supplies calcium ions (Ca²⁺), and it is used to replace calcium or, topically, to treat hydrofluoric acid exposure.

Calcium-alkali (milk-alkali) syndrome results from excessive calcium and alkali intake, so calcium gluconate is a plausible cause or aggravator, not a therapy. The high TxGNN score most likely reflects a drug-disease association in the knowledge graph, not a therapeutic effect. **This result should be read as a safety signal.**
