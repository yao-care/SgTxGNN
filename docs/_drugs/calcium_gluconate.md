## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindication-related diseases (knowledge graph, not label text)**: The knowledge graph links calcium gluconate to many conditions where it may be contraindicated. Key examples:
  - Calcium and mineral disorders: hypercalcemia disease, humoral hypercalcemia of malignancy, hyperparathyroidism, nephrocalcinosis, phosphorus metabolism disease, sarcoidosis
  - Kidney: kidney disease, impaired renal function, nephrolithiasis
  - Cardiovascular: heart disease, cardiovascular disease, hypertension, sudden cardiac arrest
  - Others: liver disease and liver failure, peptic ulcer disease, gout, epilepsy, thrombocytopenia, hemophilia
- **Drug Interactions**: 83 interactions were identified; the first 20 are shown in the compiled data.
  - **Major**: dolutegravir, bictegravir, ceftriaxone (the ceftriaxone interaction is a recognised precipitation risk with IV calcium)
  - **Moderate**: tetracyclines (doxycycline, tetracycline); bisphosphonates (alendronic acid, risedronic acid, ibandronate); calcium channel blockers (nifedipine, amlodipine, diltiazem); beta-blockers (acebutolol, atenolol, betaxolol, bisoprolol, carvedilol); hydrochlorothiazide; topical vitamin D analogues (calcipotriol, calcitriol); baloxavir marboxil

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for label safety information.
