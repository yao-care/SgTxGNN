## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, eplerenone is a selective mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist. It blocks the effects of aldosterone, which promotes sodium retention, vascular tone, inflammation and fibrosis. Its efficacy in hypertension is established, and mechanistically the same pathway may apply to pulmonary vascular disease.

A plausible link to pulmonary vascular remodelling exists through MR-mediated fibrosis, inflammation and vascular tone. Preclinical work in the related hypoxia-associated category supports this idea:

- Aldosterone synthesis is upregulated by hypoxia in pulmonary artery endothelial cells and promotes pulmonary vascular fibrosis (PMID 25001622).
- Aldosterone signalling drives abnormal pulmonary artery smooth muscle cell survival (PMID 27006450).
- Eplerenone attenuated pathological pulmonary vascular remodelling in a pulmonary arterial hypertension model, but not right ventricular remodelling (PMID 29499691).
- Treating established disease with MR antagonists improved interventricular dependence in a rat model (PMID 35043674).

This evidence is animal or cell-based. The high TxGNN score is a computational prediction only, and no human data for pulmonary hypertension were supplied.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-related cautions (knowledge-graph contraindication data, not label text)**: liver disease, gallbladder disease, diabetic nephropathy, and impaired renal function disease.
- **Drug Interactions**: 100 interactions were identified. Examples of **Major** interactions include:
  - Aprepitant
  - Clarithromycin
  - Clotrimazole
  - Potassium chloride, potassium citrate and potassium bicarbonate (raised hyperkalaemia risk)

  **Moderate** interactions include corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, prednisone and others), SGLT2 inhibitors (canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin), acetylsalicylic acid, bupropion, miconazole, and the cannabinoids dronabinol and nabilone.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and formal contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is very high and the mechanism is biologically plausible. However, there are no clinical trials or publications for this exact indication, so the evidence level is L5. Related evidence for hypoxia-associated pulmonary hypertension is preclinical (L4) and mixed, with a possible vascular benefit but doubtful right ventricular benefit.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the HSA website (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Human clinical data for eplerenone in pulmonary hypertension, such as pilot trials or case series
- A safety plan covering hyperkalaemia and renal function monitoring, given the major potassium and CYP3A4-inhibitor interactions
- Clarification of the approved indication text for the Singapore licences
