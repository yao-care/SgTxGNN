## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Natalizumab blocks alpha4-integrin-mediated movement of white blood cells (leukocyte trafficking). Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the DrugBank field of this evidence pack. This mechanism reduces immune-cell entry into inflamed tissue, which is the basis of its use in MS.

The data does not support a specific reason why this would help bronchitis. Leukocyte trafficking plays a role in airway inflammation in theory, but no study in the pack tests it. Natalizumab is also associated with infection risk, so an anti-inflammatory benefit in a respiratory infection or inflammation setting is unproven and could be harmful. The high score most likely reflects patterns in the knowledge graph rather than clinical findings.
