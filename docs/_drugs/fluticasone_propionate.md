## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, fluticasone propionate is a synthetic glucocorticoid that binds the glucocorticoid receptor (NR3C2 and PGR binding is also recorded for the mineralocorticoid and progesterone receptors). Activating this receptor suppresses inflammatory cytokines and immune cell activity.

2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate sensitization is an allergic contact sensitization, a type IV (delayed) hypersensitivity reaction. Fluticasone propionate is already used for related allergic conditions, including contact dermatitis, so a mechanistic link is plausible. However, this rationale is inferred from the model prediction only. No trials or literature were found for this specific sensitization, and the drug's similarity to the original indications has not been formally assessed.

**Other predictions in the ranking (for context):**
- **Atopic dermatitis (rank 7, score 95.3%)** is already among the drug's original indications, so it validates the model rather than representing true repurposing. It has the strongest supporting data (see below).
- **Alopecia areata (rank 5, score 95.9%)** has a plausible class-level rationale, as topical and intralesional corticosteroids are established treatments, but no fluticasone-specific evidence was found.
- **Alopecia mucinosa (rank 3)** and **folliculitis decalvans (rank 6)** have plausible anti-inflammatory rationales but no evidence.
- **Vulvar inverted follicular keratosis, telogen effluvium, vocal cord polyp, and hereditary hypotrichosis with recurrent skin vesicles** have weak or no mechanistic rationale.
- **Polyp of middle ear (rank 10)** has only indirect evidence from nasal polyposis studies.
