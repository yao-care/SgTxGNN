## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

Additional information from the knowledge graph (not label text):
- **Potential contraindicated conditions:** hypertension, long QT syndrome, epilepsy, liver disease, gallbladder disease, hyperglycaemia, measles, herpes simplex virus keratitis, and various pituitary hormone deficiency and hypogonadotropic hypogonadism conditions.
- **Receptor binding:** besides the glucocorticoid receptor, the drug binds the mineralocorticoid and progesterone receptors.
- **Literature signals:** the data includes reviews on adrenal (HPA axis) suppression with intranasal corticosteroids and on corticosteroid allergy in asthma patients.
