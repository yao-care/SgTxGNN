## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in DrugBank for this record. Fluticasone propionate is a glucocorticoid receptor agonist with anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive effects. It is already marketed in several forms, including a topical cream.

2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate (HEMA) sensitization is an allergic contact reaction. Glucocorticoids are widely used to calm allergic contact dermatitis, so a link is plausible on paper. However, this argument rests on drug class only. The prediction score (0.986) is the sole support, and no fluticasone-specific trials or publications were found for this condition.

A glucocorticoid would also treat the resulting dermatitis rather than remove the underlying sensitization. Whether "sensitization" is the right treatment target still needs clarification.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Rationale:**
The only support is a model prediction with no trials or publications, and the mechanism argument is class-level only. There is also no safety data to review.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A targeted literature and trial search for fluticasone or topical corticosteroids in HEMA or acrylate contact allergy
- Clarification of whether the clinical target is the sensitization itself or the resulting contact dermatitis
- The HSA package insert (warnings and contraindications) and the approved indication text for each registration
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Route-compatibility assessment, which is still pending
