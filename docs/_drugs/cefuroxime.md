## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the supplied record. Cefuroxime is a beta-lactam antibacterial that inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis.

Polyclonal hyperviscosity syndrome is caused by excess polyclonal immunoglobulins thickening the blood. It is not an infectious process, and cefuroxime has no known effect on immunoglobulin levels or blood viscosity.

The high score (99.76%, model rank 3858) most likely reflects a graph-based association rather than a biological link. No supporting trials or literature were retrieved, so this prediction is not credible without new evidence.
