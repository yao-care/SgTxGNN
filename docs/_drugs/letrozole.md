## Letrozole: From Breast Cancer Endocrine Therapy to Female Breast Carcinoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Letrozole is an oral aromatase inhibitor that lowers estrogen production, and it is already marketed in Singapore for hormone-driven breast cancer.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **female breast carcinoma**, which is an established use of the drug, not a new one.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, letrozole is a non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor. It blocks the conversion of androgens to estrogens, which lowers estrogen-driven growth signalling in hormone receptor-positive breast cancer.

The predicted indication is therefore consistent with how the drug is already used. Phase 3 data (MONALEESA-2, PALOMA-2, COMPLEEMENT-1) support letrozole as the endocrine backbone in combination with CDK4/6 inhibitors. Because this is an established, marketed use, it is not a repurposing candidate in the strict sense. The prediction mainly confirms current practice.

Other predictions in the data are weaker. They include:
- Hormone-resistant breast carcinoma (L2), a research question where benefit would come from combinations.
- Bilateral breast carcinoma (L3), a research question supported only by indirect evidence.
- Estrogen-receptor negative breast cancer, nipple carcinoma, breast fibrocystic disease, benign mammary dysplasia and Ehrlich tumor carcinoma (all L4–L5, Hold). For ER-negative disease, the high score is not backed by direct clinical evidence.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-level contraindication signals (knowledge graph, not label text)**: liver failure, cirrhosis of liver, osteoporosis, postmenopausal osteoporosis, hyperbilirubinemia, hereditary hyperbilirubinemia, Gilbert syndrome and cerebrovascular disorder.
- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction query found 100 records. Most are of unknown severity. The only one rated Moderate is aprepitant. Others in the sample include calcitriol, glimepiride, metformin, simvastatin, prednisone, omeprazole, pantoprazole, lansoprazole, famotidine and morphine.

Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Multiple completed Phase 3 trials and RCT publications support letrozole-based endocrine therapy in HR+ breast cancer. The drug is already marketed in Singapore with 11 valid registrations. This is confirmation of an established use, not a new repurposing opportunity, and the package insert safety review is still outstanding.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Singapore package insert warnings and contraindications (a blocking gap for safety screening)
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Approved indication text for the Singapore licences
- Bone-health and liver-function monitoring plan, given the knowledge-graph contraindication signals
- Restriction of use to hormone receptor-positive disease, since ER-negative evidence is indirect
