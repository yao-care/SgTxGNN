## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the supplied record. Solifenacin is a selective M3 muscarinic antagonist that acts on bladder detrusor smooth muscle. It reduces involuntary contractions and relieves urgency, frequency and incontinence.

This prediction has **no plausible mechanistic link** to that action. PKD3 and polycystic liver disease are driven by defects in polycystin processing and cystogenesis (GANAB/PRKCSH-related). Cyst growth in these conditions involves cAMP signalling, cell proliferation and fluid secretion, and M3 blockade has no established role in these processes.

The high TxGNN score reflects graph proximity, not biological or clinical support. None of the 20 retrieved publications (guidelines, genetics reviews and one hydatid cyst review) mention solifenacin.
