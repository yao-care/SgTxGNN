# Durvalumab: From Transitional Cell Carcinoma to Prostatic Urethra Urothelial Carcinoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Durvalumab is an anti-PD-L1 antibody originally used to treat transitional cell (urothelial) carcinoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **prostatic urethra urothelial carcinoma**, with a very high score of 99.98%.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Durvalumab is known to be an anti-PD-L1 monoclonal antibody. It blocks the PD-L1 pathway that tumours use to evade immune attack, and it is already used in urothelial carcinoma, its original indication.

Prostatic urethra urothelial carcinoma has the same urothelial histology as the original indication, so the same PD-L1 biology may apply. This is an extrapolation from histological similarity, not from direct data. The very high TxGNN score supports the prediction, but it is a computational result and not clinical proof.

For context, other predicted indications for this drug include urothelial variants with some trial activity (see "Related Evidence in Other Predicted Indications" below).

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 81 interactions were recorded. Examples are listed below.
  - **Major**: Adalimumab
  - **Moderate**: corticosteroids (hydrocortisone, triamcinolone, dexamethasone, betamethasone, budesonide, prednisolone, prednisone, methylprednisolone, deflazacort), roflumilast, alefacept, alemtuzumab, anakinra, tetanus toxoid and anthrax vaccines

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests on histological similarity to the approved urothelial indication and a high model score. No trials or publications address prostatic urethra urothelial carcinoma specifically, and warnings and contraindications from the package insert are not available. Other urothelial variants (sarcomatoid bladder and upper-tract carcinoma) have early-phase trials and could be a more evidence-supported research focus.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Site-specific clinical or observational data, or inclusion of prostatic urethra involvement in existing urothelial trials
- The Singapore package insert, covering approved indication, warnings and contraindications
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- Review of how existing pivotal urothelial trial results apply to this tumour site
