## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Clinical Pharmacology section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for benzylpenicillin: "Penicillins bind to serum proteins, mainly albumin. Therapeutic levels of the penicillins are easily achieved under normal circumstances in extracellular fluid and most other body tissues. Penicillins are distributed in varying degrees into pleural, pericardial, peritoneal, ascitic, synovial, and interstitial fluids." ([DailyMed: Pfizerpen (penicillin g potassium) prescribing information, Roerig](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=efef4846-9497-41af-8dd6-518f86eca7f2), label version 2026-11-04; accessed 2026-11-19.) Benzylpenicillin is a penicillin-class antibiotic, and penicillins are generally active against susceptible Gram-positive bacteria such as streptococci.

Pericoronitis is a polymicrobial infection (streptococci and anaerobes) around a partially erupted tooth. Because benzylpenicillin is active against many of these organisms, the prediction is biologically plausible. However, the data compiled for this report contain no trials or literature on this disease, and the high score is a model output only.

There are also practical concerns. Benzylpenicillin is marketed in Singapore only as an injectable powder, and its limited oral bioavailability makes it a less natural fit for a typically localised dental infection. Whether an injectable route is appropriate for this condition has not been assessed.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 21 interactions were found in the data compiled for this report. Key ones:
  - **Major**: Methotrexate (penicillins can reduce methotrexate clearance, so toxicity monitoring is needed).
  - **Moderate**: Warfarin, dicoumarol, tetracycline-class antibiotics (doxycycline, minocycline, tetracycline, demeclocycline, oxytetracycline), chloramphenicol, ethinylestradiol, mycophenolic acid, picosulfuric acid, radioactive iodide (I-123, I-131).
  - **Minor**: Clarithromycin, erythromycin.
  - **Level unknown**: Salbutamol, acyclovir, gentamicin, levothyroxine.
- **Conditions of concern in the data**: Kidney disease, pyoureter, *Clostridium difficile* colitis, perinatal necrotizing enterocolitis, infectious mononucleosis and chronic Epstein-Barr virus infection syndrome are recorded as conditions where use may be a concern. These are not taken from label text and should be checked against the package insert.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps
