## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions:** The data compiled for this report list pharmacological target interactions only, with no specific interacting drugs or severity levels. Dosulepin acts on the muscarinic receptors M1–M5, the histamine H1 receptor, the noradrenaline transporter and the serotonin transporter. Its anticholinergic and sedative effects, and its additive effects with other drugs acting on these targets, should be considered.
- **Cardiac safety:** Studies of patients on dosulepin report changes in QT dispersion, indicating cardiotoxicity (PMIDs 15311996, 15354946). The wider antidepressant literature also flags a sudden cardiac death risk (PMID 20139216).
- **Special populations:** Use in infants would raise major safety concerns, including anticholinergic effects and cardiac toxicity.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.
