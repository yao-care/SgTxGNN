## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications recorded in the knowledge graph** (not taken from package insert text): liver failure, anxiety disorder, neurotic disorder, dysthymic disorder.
- **Class-related risks from the literature**: cognitive impairment, tolerance, rebound insomnia on discontinuation, falls, car accidents, abuse and dependence. Residual next-day effects on driving are also described. Use is generally recommended for no longer than four weeks.
- **Drug interactions**: The interaction query returned 100 records, but none has a severity grade (all are "Unknown"). Examples include morphine, metformin, omeprazole, pantoprazole, prednisone and epinephrine. Interaction severity cannot be judged from this data. Morphine is the one example that deserves attention given likely additive central nervous system depression, but this is general pharmacological reasoning rather than a graded finding.

Please refer to the package insert for full warnings and contraindications.
