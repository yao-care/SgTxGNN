# Vitamin E: From Vitamin Supplementation to Inborn Disorder of Bilirubin Metabolism

## One-Sentence Summary

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble vitamin and antioxidant. In Singapore it is registered in vitamin products for injection and oral use, and the registration records give no indication text.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **inborn disorder of bilirubin metabolism**, but **none of the 3 retrieved clinical trials and neither of the 2 publications** tested vitamin E for this condition.
This prediction rests on knowledge-graph proximity only.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Vitamin E is a lipid-soluble antioxidant that limits lipid peroxidation. It is used clinically to replace vitamin E in people who cannot absorb fat-soluble vitamins.

No direct link between vitamin E and bilirubin metabolism was shown. The only plausible link is indirect. Cholestatic liver disease and bile acid synthesis disorders impair absorption of fat-soluble vitamins, including vitamin E, so patients may need vitamin E replacement. This is correction of a nutritional deficiency, not treatment of the underlying disorder.

The very high TxGNN score (99.99%) reflects closeness in the knowledge graph. It is not clinical evidence. The prediction should be treated as a hypothesis to test.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has no supporting trial or publication specific to vitamin E, and the only plausible link is nutritional replacement in cholestasis, not treatment of the bilirubin disorder itself. The drug is already marketed in Singapore, but there is no evidence to justify a new-indication pursuit.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the HSA records, which are required before any safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data (for example from DrugBank)
- Approved indication text for the three Singapore registrations, to confirm the original indication
- A targeted literature review of vitamin E in neonatal and cholestatic hyperbilirubinaemia. One 1979 study of vitamin E and neonatal bilirubinaemia was found under the related "bilirubin metabolism disease" prediction, but its design and results could not be confirmed from the available data
