# Zolbetuximab: From Gastric Cancer to Diabetic Cataract

## One-Sentence Summary

Zolbetuximab is a monoclonal antibody against claudin-18.2 (CLDN18.2), a protein found in gastric epithelium and gastric/gastro-oesophageal junction tumours. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **diabetic cataract**, with a very high score but **no clinical trials and no publications** supporting this direction. This is a model prediction only, and no biological link was found.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Zolbetuximab is an antibody directed at CLDN18.2, a tight-junction protein. Its known role is in gastric epithelium and gastric/GEJ tumours. It has no known role in lens biology, polyol-pathway activity or diabetic lens opacification.

The prediction does not look biologically grounded. The top 10 predictions are all eye conditions: nine cataract subtypes and diabetic retinopathy. Seven of them share nearly identical scores (0.984), which suggests a similarity effect within the cataract disease cluster in the knowledge graph rather than a signal specific to one indication. A systemic IgG1 antibody aimed at a gastric target is also unlikely to reach the avascular lens. The same applies to diabetic retinopathy, which is driven by VEGF, inflammation and microvascular dysfunction, none of which zolbetuximab targets.

The original mechanism and indication fields could not be verified from the data supplied, so the prediction could not be checked against known pharmacology beyond the points above.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Rationale:**

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
