## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: The interaction screen was completed. It returned four pharmacological target entries rather than drug-drug interactions: JAK1, JAK2, JAK3 and TYK2.
- Causality is uncertain.
- **Regulatory Note**: Public reports state the FDA issued a complete response letter in 2020 because of concerns about the effect of the 200 mg dose on sperm concentration. The EMA and Japan's PMDA approved filgotinib for rheumatoid arthritis.

Please refer to the package insert for full warnings and contraindications.
